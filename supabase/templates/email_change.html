Someone asked to make {{ .NewEmail }} the sign-in email of a Vibe account. Vibe does not change sign-in emails that way.

To change your own, write to the Vibe team from the email you sign in with now. The Vibe Privacy Policy gives the address.

Did not ask, and this came to the email you sign in with? Someone may be signed in as you. Open Vibe, go to Settings and tap "Sign out of other devices". Then tap Sign out, and on the Log in page tap "Forgot?" next to Password to choose a new password.

Did not ask, and it came to any other email? Ignore it. Nothing changes.
