# What "scaling" means for Vibe — November 2026 to spring 2027

Written 2026-09-25 for the founders' meeting (Franky, James, Rylan). Plain English on purpose.
Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** still needs checking. Targets marked **(our suggestion)** are not facts —
they are starting numbers for the meeting to argue about and then lock in.

---

## 1. The short answer

Scaling this winter does **not** mean "more campuses fast."
It means: **make Indianapolis full and busy first, then open one more campus — only when the numbers say so.**

- November to January: Indianapolis only. Clubs, students, and keeping it safe.
- February to April: if Indianapolis passes the test in section 4, run a small test at one new campus.
- August 2027 (welcome week): the real launch at campus number two.

One campus that loves Vibe beats five campuses where it feels empty.

## 2. How other campus apps grew (and what went wrong for one of them)

| App | What they did | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook (2004)** | Harvard only at first. About 5,055 Harvard students joined in the first month. Then it opened to three schools. Each school could only see its own students. Columbia had 37 profiles and Stanford 26 in their first days — new campuses start small. | Harvard Crimson, Mar 1, 2004 — https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2004/3/1/facebook-expands-beyond-harvard-harvard-students/ |
| **Facebook** | Went only to "carefully chosen" colleges and had 10,000 users a month in. | Harvard Magazine, accessed 2026-09-25 — https://www.harvardmagazine.com/harvard-facebook-history-mark-zuckerberg-winklevoss |
| **Tinder (2012)** | Started at USC. The founders pitched sororities and fraternities in person. | Lenny Rachitsky, May 12, 2020 — https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-the-biggest-consumer-apps-got |
| **Tinder** | A party you could only enter with the app installed. About 500 very connected people were enough for the whole campus. Then they copied the plan campus by campus. | Startup Archive (quoting Andrew Chen's Dec 2021 talk), Aug 20, 2023 — https://www.startuparchive.org/p/three-case-studies-from-andrew-chen-on-solving-the-cold-start-problem |
| **Fizz** | Launches on the **first day of classes**. Paid student helpers (about $50–60 per shift) handed out free donuts for downloads. | The Ithacan, Sept 20, 2023 — https://theithacan.org/49642/news/fizz-hires-ic-students-to-market-app-on-campus/ |
| **Fizz** | Recruits local student moderators at each campus. Now grows when students ask for it at their school. **UNVERIFIED** counts: about 240 colleges and 4,000 volunteer moderators. | Forbes, Apr 18, 2025 (page blocked; search summary only) — https://www.forbes.com/sites/ianshepherd/2025/04/18/gen-zs-underground-social-network-just-went-national-and-its-blowing-up/ |
| **BeReal (2022)** | A paid college helper program and parties where getting in meant downloading the app and adding friends. **UNVERIFIED** pay details. | BestColleges, Apr 22, 2022 — https://www.bestcolleges.com/news/2022/04/22/social-network-bereal-college-students/ ; Contrary Research (accessed 2026-09-25) — https://research.contrary.com/company/bereal |
| **Sidechat (2022)** | Took off at a few schools first (Harvard, Cornell), school email only, then spread. | Harvard Crimson, Apr 12, 2022 — https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2022/4/12/sidechat-social-media-app-takes-off-at-harvard/ |
| **Yik Yak — the warning** | Spread fast across campuses. Bullying and threats followed. Several colleges blocked it on campus Wi-Fi. By 2016 it laid off about 60% of staff; it shut down in April 2017. | Inside Higher Ed, Dec 18/19, 2016 — https://www.insidehighered.com/news/2016/12/19/students-lose-interest-yik-yak-relief-administrators ; Inside Higher Ed, Mar 7, 2022 — https://www.insidehighered.com/news/2022/03/07/yik-yak-re-emerges-after-shutdown |

**What this teaches Vibe, in five lines:**
1. **Win one place all the way first.** Facebook stayed at Harvard for a month. Tinder stayed at USC.
2. **Go in through groups that already exist.** Tinder used sororities and fraternities. Vibe's plan uses clubs. Same idea.
3. **Launch on a big day.** First day of classes or welcome week. Not a random Tuesday.
4. **Have a real person on the ground at each campus.** Fizz, BeReal and Tinder all did.
5. **Safety before speed.** Yik Yak grew faster than it could keep people safe, and it died.

## 4. The "ready for the next campus" test

Open a second campus only when **all six** are true **four weeks in a row**. All targets are **(our suggestion)**.

| # | Test | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Active clubs** at Indianapolis | **50 clubs** that posted or listed an event in the last 14 days (about 1 in 7 clubs) | Clubs are the reason students open Vibe. |
| 2 | **Weekly active students** | **1,000** students who opened Vibe in the last 7 days (about 4% of the campus) | Tinder needed ~500 connected people for USC. We aim a bit higher. |
| 3 | **Students come back** | **1 in 3** students who joined 4 weeks ago used Vibe this week | Good social apps keep ~25% after 6 months (great: 45%). Lenny Rachitsky, June 2020 — https://x.com/lennysan/status/1277620704146423809 . A 4-week number should be higher than that. |
| 4 | **Clubs run themselves** | At least **half** of this week's club posts and events were made by club officers, not by us | If we stop pushing and it stops, it isn't ready. |
| 5 | **Safety keeps up** | Every report handled within **24 hours**, and no founder is burned out | The Yik Yak lesson. The new Terms (with the lawyer) are set to promise a 24-hour review. |
| 6 | **The next campus is asking** | **150+** students from that campus signed up, plus **one student there agreed to lead** it | Fizz grows where students ask for it. |

Good news for test 6: sign-up already lets a student pick a campus that is marked **"not open yet"**
(`src/components/mobile/onboarding/StepCampus.tsx`). Those picks are a free waiting list. Count them monthly.

If the test is not passed by early February, **that is fine**. Keep working on Indianapolis and aim the
second campus at August 2027 welcome week instead.

## 5. Which campus is next, and why

Vibe already knows 11 campuses (`src/lib/iu/campuses.ts`). Only Indianapolis is open. Opening another one is a
**small change on our side** (one switch in the code, plus a majors list) — not a new app. The hard part is people, not code.

| Order | Campus | Why | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st (lean)** | **IU Bloomington** | Same @iu.edu email, so sign-up already works. A Bloomington majors list is already built (`src/lib/iu/majors-bloomington.ts`). Biggest IU campus; 750–900+ student groups (https://bloomington.iu.edu/campus-life/community/student-organizations.html, accessed 2026-09-25). About an hour's drive (**UNVERIFIED**, check a map). Many Indy students know people there. | It is huge. Don't try to win "all of Bloomington." Go in through **one slice** — Greek life, or one school like Kelley or Luddy. |
| **2nd (close)** | **Purdue West Lafayette** | Purdue calls its Indianapolis location part of West Lafayette: same courses, same degrees (The Campus Citizen, URL dated Nov 2025 — https://www.thecampuscitizen.com/article/2025/11/purdue-in-indianapolis-tries-to-embody-spirit-of-west-lafayette-campus). 55,079 students this fall (Based in Lafayette, accessed 2026-09-25 — https://www.basedinlafayette.com/p/purdue-enrollment-up-just-shy-of). 1,000+ clubs (Purdue, accessed 2026-09-25 — https://www.purdue.edu/sao/get-involved/index.php). | Needs a West Lafayette majors list (not built). Also huge — same "one slice" rule. |
| 3rd | **Fort Wayne** (IU + Purdue share it, like Indy) | Vibe already treats it as a shared campus, closed for now. Purdue Fort Wayne has 5,724 students (Purdue, Sept 14, 2026). Smaller = easier to fill. | Less payoff. Good backup if the big campuses feel too big. |
| Later | IU East, Kokomo, Northwest, South Bend, Southeast, Columbus; Purdue Northwest | Smaller schools. | **UNVERIFIED:** many students commute, so club life may be thinner. |

**How to decide between Bloomington and West Lafayette (our suggestion):** pick the one with

## 8. When to turn on Vibe+ selling

Vibe+ is $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year. It sells more profile customization and **seeing who viewed you**
(everyone sees the count; only Vibe+ sees the names). Turn it on only when **all four** are true:

1. **The LLC has its bank account and tax number (EIN), and Stripe is connected to them.** (James)
2. **The Terms with subscription and auto-renew wording are published.** (James + lawyer)
3. **Launch has calmed down:** 2–3 weeks after Oct 31 with no big fires. **(our suggestion)**
4. **There are enough people for "who viewed you" to mean something:** about 300 weekly active students. **(our suggestion)**
   With too few people, a buyer sees an empty list and wants a refund.

**Earliest realistic date:** mid-to-late November 2026, if the paperwork is done. Otherwise January.

**Sell on the website only.** Vibe+ is hidden in the store apps at launch (decided Sept 24).
- Apple takes **15%** of in-app subscriptions for small companies (Apple Small Business Program, accessed 2026-09-25 —
  https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/).
- US rules on linking from an app to a website checkout are still in court. The Supreme Court took Apple's appeal;
  arguments are unlikely before 2027 (Tech Times, Sept 15, 2026 —
  https://www.techtimes.com/articles/327527/20260915/app-store-commission-limbo-enters-new-phase-apples-epic-merits-brief-opens-scotus-fight.htm).
  Exact grant date **UNVERIFIED**. **Keep links out of the apps until the lawyer says yes.**

## 9. The store apps' job

- **Students expect a real app.** It makes Vibe look real.
- **Push notifications live there.** They are the #1 way people come back. On iPhone, web push only works
  after "Add to Home Screen," so the store app matters.
- **One app for every campus.** Opening campus two needs no new app and no new Apple review.
- **They won't find students for us.** **(our view)** Growth still comes from clubs and friends.
- **Deadline:** last safe submission is Fri Oct 16. If Apple is late, the website is the backup.

## 11. What NOT to do yet

- **No paid ads** (Instagram or TikTok ads). None of the big consumer apps in Lenny Rachitsky's study got their
  first 1,000 users from ads (May 12, 2020 — https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-the-biggest-consumer-apps-got).
  An ad sends a student to an app that still feels empty; they leave and the money is gone.
  **Revisit** only after campus two passes the test and 4-week return is above 1 in 3. Free posting and teasers stay on.
- **No hiring and no paid campus reps.** No payroll, no contracts.
  Before anyone outside the three founders does work for Vibe — even for free or for perks — James asks the lawyer.
  US labor rules say unpaid work is generally fine for nonprofits and government, but Vibe is a for-profit company
  (US Dept. of Labor Fact Sheet #71, updated Jan 2018 — https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/71-flsa-internships).
- **No second campus before the test passes.** And never two new campuses at once.
- **No new big features.** Video posts stay parked. The recruiter "snapshot" stays out. Polish what exists.
- **No deals with the universities yet.** Be useful to clubs first. **(our view)**
- **No high schools and no "IU Online" campus.**
