# Fact-check: ambassadors.md

Checked 2026-09-25 against the original pages (company, school, government and news sites). I did not edit `ambassadors.md`. Rule used: no original source means **UNVERIFIED**.

## The short version

- **The plan holds up.** 24 of the claims I checked are right, including every school-policy detail, the FTC rules and the Sidechat, Fizz, Tinder and Facebook stories.
- **Four fixes change what we actually do:**
  1. **Purdue's fall break is Mon–Tue Oct 12–13, 2026,** which falls in **Week 3** ("Clubs first"). IU Indianapolis's is Mon–Tue **Oct 19–20** (Week 4). The plan only slows down for Week 4, so Purdue clubs will be on break in the week we need them most.
  2. **The labor-law question is harder than the plan says.** The Department of Labor says plainly: "employees may not volunteer services to for-profit private sector employers." The "who benefits more" test in the plan is for **internships**, not for unpaid promoters. Calling the group a "founding members group" does not change how the law treats it. James should ask the lawyer that exact question.
  3. **Purdue in Indianapolis is not a "regional campus."** Since July 1, 2024 it has been part of the West Lafayette campus. The Purdue policy still applies, because it covers "all real property owned or occupied by Purdue," but the "regional campuses" wording in the plan is the wrong hook.
  4. **Purdue's Indianapolis student-life office is now known:** StudentLifeIndy@purdue.edu, ET 331, 765-495-7797. That fills in the "office UNVERIFIED" gap in Week 1.
- **Six smaller fixes are about credit and wording:** Fizz's 95% figure comes from its own founder. BeReal's 2023 drop comes from outside estimates, and BeReal disputed them. Tinder's "under 5,000" and Snapchat's numbers aren't in the articles cited for them (the Snapchat numbers are in a better Forbes article). Robinhood's million comes from its own CEO, not just growth blogs. The Gopuff listing doesn't mention group messages.

## Still UNVERIFIED (no original source answers these)

- Whether an LLC can legally run a perks-only ambassador group, and whether free Vibe+ counts as pay. **Lawyer question** (see fix 2).
- Whether a free app counts as "commercial solicitation" under IU FIN-PURCH-12 or Purdue IV.B.3. Only the schools can answer.
- Whether IU or Purdue will let a founder present at a club meeting held in a campus room.
- Residence Life and Orientation rules for RAs and orientation leaders who promote an outside company.
- Prices for stickers and a pizza night.
- BeReal's real 2023 daily users (outside estimates and the company disagree).
- Tinder's "under 5,000" pre-trip number, from an original source.
- How Handshake used student reps early on (the plan already leaves this out).
- The planning numbers ("about 2 hours a week," "100–200 students," "no more than about 12 people"). These are guesses for us to test, not facts.
