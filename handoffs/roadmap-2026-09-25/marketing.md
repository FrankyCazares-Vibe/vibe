# Vibe marketing plan: Sep 26 to Oct 31, then November

Written 2026-09-25 for the founders' meeting on Sep 26.
Owners: **Rylan** (most of it), **Franky** (the app, landing page, store pages), **James** (help@ inbox, legal, money).
Sources are tagged like [S4]. The full list with links and dates is at the bottom.

Words used here:
- **Store listing** = our page in the App Store or Google Play.
- **Verified student** = someone who proved they're a student with a school email.
- **Weekly active** = opened Vibe in the last 7 days.
- **QR code** = the square code a phone camera scans.

## The short version

- **Clubs first.** No big push to students until at least 10 clubs have a page and real events on Vibe.
- **Launch week is Halloween week:** Mon Oct 26 to Sat Oct 31. Launch night is Thu Oct 29.
- **One link everywhere: connectvibe.app/get.** Today it sends phones to the web app. The day a store listing is set, it sends that phone to the store instead. Flyers printed now never go out of date.
- **Three posts a week:** "This week on campus", "Meet the club", "Building Vibe".
- **Only claim what's live.** No fake numbers. No fake users. No features we haven't shipped.
- **Five numbers every Sunday:** sign-ups, verified students, clubs live, events posted, weekly active students.
- **Biggest risk:** IU's rules on flyers and handouts. Rylan asks the campus offices this week (section 4).

## 1. The story in one sentence

> **"Vibe is where IU Indy and Purdue Indy students find their clubs, their events, and each other, all in one place."**

Short version for a bio or sticker: **"Your campus, all in one place."**

Why this line:
- It names our campus. Local beats general.
- It leads with clubs and events. That's what will be on the app on day one.
- "Each other" covers profiles, following and messages without promising more.

Careful with school names. We say "IU Indy" and "Purdue Indy" in words, to describe who it's for.
We never use their logos, colors, mascots or building photos.
IU says its names and marks need a license [S19, S20], so James asks the lawyer if our wording is fine (UNVERIFIED until then).
Landing page and flyers carry one line: "Vibe is not affiliated with Indiana University or Purdue University."

## 2. Five rules for every post, flyer and DM

1. **Only claim what's live.** If it isn't on connectvibe.app today, we don't show it or hint at it. Right now that means:
   - No "get notified" promises. Phone notifications are built but switched off.
   - No "Download on the App Store" until the listing is really live. Apple says the badge is only for apps that are on the store [S17].
   - No Vibe+ (the paid plan). Billing is off.
2. **Real people, real events, real numbers.** Never make fake accounts or fake events to look busy. Never round up.
   Fizz told the press 95% of Stanford undergrads had downloaded it. TechCrunch pointed out free donuts for downloads probably pumped that up [S5].
   We'd rather say "12 clubs are on Vibe" and have it be true.
3. **Ask before you post someone.** Get a clear yes before a student's face or a club's page goes in a video.
4. **One link.** connectvibe.app/get on everything. Store badges get added the day a listing goes live, not before.
5. **Clubs first.** See section 6.

## 3. What worked for other campus apps

| What they did | Source | What we copy |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook started at one school and got 1,200 to 1,500 sign-ups in its first day by posting to dorm email lists. | Lenny Rachitsky, May 12 2020 [S1] | Win one campus deeply. Don't think about a second campus yet. |
| Tinder's early team gave talks at sorority meetings and had everyone install it in the room, then went to the partner fraternity. It also threw a USC party you could only get into with the app. | What a Startup, Sep 20 2024 [S4]; [S1] | Rylan asks for 3 minutes at each club's weekly meeting. Members sign up in the room. Launch night entry = your Vibe profile on your phone. |
| Start with a small group where everyone knows each other. 100 connected people beat 1,000 strangers. | Andrew Chen, Mar 2014 [S3]; Lenny's Newsletter, Dec 9 2021 [S2] | Our first group is 10 clubs and their members, not "all students". |
| Fizz slid flyers with QR codes under dorm doors and handed out donuts. It grew fast, but its numbers got questioned. | TechCrunch, Oct 4 2022 [S5] | QR flyers and small freebies work. Bragging with shaky numbers backfires. |
| Ditto (a 2025 college dating app) grows with events, campus reps, cheap surprise stunts, and videos. A cofounder lived on campus for a week. Its events teach the app, not just hand out free stuff. | Playkit, Dec 10 2025 [S6]; GlobeNewswire, Feb 3 2026 [S7] | Launch night shows Vibe on a big screen: the week's events and the clubs on it. |
| Campus rep programs work best after people already keep using the app. Pick one campus lead by referral, add 2 to 3 helpers, check numbers weekly. | Future (a16z), May 18 2022 [S8] | No paid rep program before launch. Rylan is the campus lead. Look again in mid-November. |
| Founder-on-camera videos beat polished ads for new startups. A new TikTok account can still reach people, because TikTok shows videos by interest, not by follower count. | Stackmatix, 2026 [S9] | Franky and Rylan on camera every week. Rough is fine. |

## 4. Campus rules to check first (this week)

This is the biggest risk in the plan.

- **IU bans commercial flyers, chalking and handouts on campus without approval.** That includes sidewalks and lawns.
  Exceptions need approval in advance from IU Procurement Services, and at IU Indy also the student affairs office [S10].
  Vibe is a company (CONNECTVIBE. LLC), so even free flyers probably count. How they treat a free app made by students: UNVERIFIED. We have to ask.
- **Campus Center tables:** clubs book free info tables through The Spot. Outside groups email the Campus Center. Plan on 7 days' notice. Handouts only from behind a booked table [S11].
- **Campus Center screens and posters** are only for registered clubs and IU departments [S12].
- **Purdue in Indianapolis** may have its own rules. UNVERIFIED.

What we do about it:
1. **Rylan emails campcntr@iu.edu and the CUBE (stuact@iu.edu) by Mon Sep 28.** Something like: "We're students with a free campus app. How can we table and put up flyers the right way?" Book the earliest table they allow.
2. **Until we get a yes, printed flyers only go off campus**: nearby coffee shops and student apartment buildings, with the owner's OK. Or hand them out at partner clubs' own meetings, when the club invites us.
3. **Clubs can add "RSVP on Vibe" and our QR code to their own event posts.** That's a club promoting its own event. Ask the CUBE if that's fine on printed posters (UNVERIFIED).
4. **Online has none of these limits**: Instagram, TikTok, club group chats, club Instagram stories.

## 5. Three kinds of posts, every week

Post on Instagram (Reels and Stories) and TikTok. Same video on both.
Aim for 3 posts a week, plus Stories most days.

**A. "This week on campus" (Monday). It's useful.**
- The 5 best events this week, taken from events clubs posted on Vibe. Ends with "RSVP on Vibe, link in bio."
- Example: a swipe-through post, "5 things to do at IU Indy this week." Each slide is a screenshot of the event on Vibe, with the club tagged.
- Launch week version: "Every Halloween event on campus, in one place."
- Only real events that are on Vibe. If there are 3, post 3.

**B. "Meet the club" (Wednesday). It's about people.**
- 30 to 45 seconds with a club officer: who they are and their next event. Then a quick scroll through their club page on Vibe: about, upcoming events, the follow button.
- Tag the club. They share it to their story, so their members see it.
- Only clubs that said yes and whose page is live.

**C. "Building Vibe" (Friday). It's about us.**
- Founders on camera, rough and honest. Franky: "why I'm building this." Rylan: "we visited 6 clubs this week, here's what they said."
- Quick campus questions: "How do you find out what's going on here?" Get a yes on camera before posting anyone.
- One real feature per week, 20 seconds each:
  - Making your profile yours (customization).
  - The campus map: where people and clubs cluster by major.
  - Following a club.
  - Messaging a friend.
- **Countdown instead of a waitlist.** From Oct 19, every Story has an Instagram countdown sticker set to Mon Oct 26. People tap "Remind me" and Instagram pings them when it hits zero [S15]. No new build needed.
- Later: Instagram's "trial reels" test a video on non-followers first [S21]. Some sites say you need 1,000 followers to get it (UNVERIFIED).

We don't have a waitlist, and don't need one. Signing up at connectvibe.app is the list.
Whether sign-up stays open to everyone before Oct 26 is a decision for tomorrow (section 13).

## 6. The club-first rule

**A student who opens Vibe for the first time should see clubs they know and events they could go to this week.**
An empty app on day one is how most campus apps die [S2, S3].

How it works (agreed Sep 21):
1. Rylan lists target clubs. IU Indy has 300+ student clubs [S13]. Purdue Indy clubs are on BoilerLink [S16].
2. **Demo day:** meet the officers in person. Show Vibe on a phone. 10 minutes.
3. If they say yes, build their club page **with them, in the same visit.** Never before they agree.
4. Copy their public upcoming events from The Spot or BoilerLink onto Vibe while they watch. Only for clubs that agreed.
5. Each club has **at least 3 upcoming events** on Vibe before Oct 26.
6. Each club gets the launch kit (section 9).

**The gate.** On Fri Oct 23 we check: 10 clubs live, 3 events each?
If not, we still launch, but smaller: only to members of the clubs that are live, with no big public push.
We never fill the app with filler.

## 9. The club launch kit (Rylan, ready by Oct 19)

- A Story graphic with the club's name, "We're on Vibe", and the QR code to connectvibe.app/get.
- A caption they can paste.
- A 3-minute talk for their meeting: what Vibe is, sign up with your school email, follow us, RSVP to our next event.
- One card with the steps: open the link, sign up with your school email, verify it, follow the club.

**The number that matters most is weekly active.** Sign-ups show our marketing works. Weekly active shows Vibe works.
