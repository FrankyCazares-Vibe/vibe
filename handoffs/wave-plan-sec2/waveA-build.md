- **Your migration's version is fixed below** and matches the production apply order. If you find it must go on the other side of the deploy, keep the number and say so in your return; the orchestrator renumbers.
- **Don't touch** `src/app/api/posts/[id]/route.ts` DELETE/PATCH, `src/lib/profile/storage-purge.ts`, `src/app/api/me/profile-upload/route.ts` (wave B).
- **Copy** is plain English; refusals never name a block ("You can't join this club right now.", "You can't add this person to this chat.").

## Product calls (made from the critics' recommendations; Franky can override before the migrations go live)

1. Hiding a widget really hides it: bio, skills, experience and working-on reach others only through the profile page. The direct data-API read of those four is revoked for everyone. Moderators see what visitors see.
2. The DM inbox subtitle is school, else blank (no bio fallback).
3. Public club posts keep their link-preview text; removed posts, hidden clubs and restricted authors get the generic "Post · Vibe" card. A restricted user's `/profile/<h>` preview is the unknown-handle preview; a hidden bio is left out of `og:description`.
4. Résumés are downloadable only by school-verified viewers, only when the owner is not restricted and the layout doesn't hide the résumé. The owner always sees their own.
5. A blocked person's comments on your posts disappear for everyone. Reports keep working across a block.
6. Adding someone to a group chat who has a block with anyone already in it is refused with "You can't add this person to this chat."; group add honours the 50-person cap.
7. Counts ("N people saved / viewed") include people you muted or blocked; only the Vibe+ name list leaves them out. So the number never moves when you mute someone.
8. Club events: at most 14 days long, can't start in the past, only verified non-hidden clubs publish, and management follows the CURRENT owner/admins (mods moderate chat, not content). Deleting a club deletes its events. An event made by an officer who is later suspended keeps today's behaviour.
9. Blocks and clubs are one-directional. If an owner/officer blocked the student, Join/Request answers "You can't join this club right now." If the student blocked an officer, they may still join or request, and their request is hidden from that officer. A victim is never locked out because their harasser is an officer.
10. A removed member must send a request to come back (no one-tap rejoin). Officers see "Removed before" on that request. Removal also drops their RSVPs to the club's upcoming events and their club-chat access; a voluntary leave drops club-chat access (not RSVPs).
11. Only the owner can demote or remove an admin, in the database too.
12. Chat reactions keep showing who reacted, to chat members (normal in chat apps). Decided, not a gap.

## Rulings after the build (2026-09-28, orchestrator) — the integration pass implements these

13. **Blocks and club posts:** a block hides the posts a person WROTE, club posts included, on every surface (A2's RLS already does this on cookie-client reads). So the service-role club grid (`src/app/orgs/[handle]/page.tsx` readOrgPosts) and `src/app/api/orgs/[slug]/profile/route.ts` filter authors with `loadHiddenUsers` for the viewer. Other officers' posts stay visible; joining is unaffected (call 9).
14. **Invites (call 9 applied):** refuse an invite when the SENDER and the invitee are a block pair (either way), or when ANY officer (owner included) blocked the invitee. Do NOT refuse because the invitee blocked some other officer or the owner: a victim is never locked out of an invite-only club by their harasser.
15. **org_members_delete narrows to self-leave only.** Every officer removal goes through the service-role route, which records the removal; the database officer branch was only a way to remove someone without a record. (Owner-only admin rule stays on UPDATE.)
16. **Mods can't approve a "Removed before" request** (owner/admin only) — as built. Removal drops RSVPs to club events that haven't ended (in-progress included) — as built. Suspended officers are gated (`requireNotRestricted`) on approve/deny and invite send too.
17. **Channels INSERT** is revoked from authenticated in `20260928150000` (every app insert is service-role; the direct path skipped the word filter), if a grep confirms no cookie-client insert.
18. **Wave B migrations move to `20260929*`.** B2 restates `posts_update_own` with A5b's club clause word for word and must not pin the old posts SELECT text (fingerprints in `crosscheck-waveA.md` §7).
