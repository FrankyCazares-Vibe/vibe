## What all six share

- One accent colour on a near-black or near-white page. Vibe already has this (coral on `#0C0B0B`).
- Very large type as the main picture. Little or no decoration.
- The menu and the main button float at the bottom of the screen (three of six).
- The work is shown as big real screenshots, never drawings.
- The four that work best say who they are in the first line. The two that make you play first (Pesselev, Behfar) are the most memorable and the slowest to understand.

## Three directions

**A. Stacked cards (Elena + Marcus).** One-line hero, then three folder cards that pile up as you scroll: Clubs, Events, Each other. Each holds one real phone screen and two lines. Bottom pill with Sign up. Clearest, best on a phone, about 2 days.

**B. Big type, screens drifting through (Marcus + gertix).** The headline fills the screen in soft Fraunces; real screens slide across it as you scroll; a ticker strip; the sentence lights up word by word. More motion and more "designed", about 3 days, needs care on slow phones.

**C. The campus at night (Behfar + Pesselev).** Keep today's stars, but make them the campus: glowing dots are clubs and events, tap one and a real screen opens. Most memorable, about 4 to 5 days, and the risk is today's problem again (a game before the explanation).

**Recommendation: A's structure with B's type and bottom pill, and C only as the hero's backdrop** (a slow coral particle bloom behind the headline, nothing to tap). The headline and the Sign up button are readable before anything moves.
