## 12. Enforcement, suspensions, bans, and appeals

If you break these Terms, we may do one or more of: remove or hide content or a single photo; remove your badge; remove your club role, or hide or hand over the club; suspend your account; ban your account. We decide which based on what happened, whether it happened before, and the risk to other people. Sexual content involving minors, intimate images shared without consent, credible threats, and getting around a ban lead to a ban the first time.

**What a suspension is.** For a set number of days (1 to 365), the only things your account can open are a notice saying what happened, why, and when it ends, and the legal pages; your profile, posts and comments are hidden from other people; nothing is deleted; Vibe+ keeps running. You can still sign out and delete your account.

**What a ban is.** The same, for good. And: the **school email verified on the account can never be verified on another Vibe account**. We keep a one-way scrambled record of that address (a keyed hash — it cannot be turned back into the address) for exactly this purpose, and it survives your deleting the account. A ban ends Vibe+ with no refund. A ban does not stop you signing in, so that you can read the notice, appeal, and delete your account and data.
