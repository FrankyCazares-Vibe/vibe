## MISSING: things the code does that the policy does not say

1. **An email typed into the admin search leaves the page.** Looking an account up by email sends the address in the request address: `src/app/admin/users-client.tsx:83` builds `/api/admin/users?limit=25&q=<address>`, read at `src/app/api/admin/users/route.ts:60`. Section 6 says Vercel's request logs keep the address requested, so the student's email lands in the host's log. Sentry's scrubber removes sign-in tokens and handles but not a `q` value (`src/lib/sentry-config.ts:20-116`), so a sampled trace or an error breadcrumb can carry it too. Section 1 says the result "never shows the address", which is true, and stops there. Best fix is in code (send the search as a POST body); otherwise section 1 needs a sentence.

2. **Three more things admins can see.** (a) The posts of a hidden club, in the feed and on the club page (`src/app/api/feed/route.ts:705-732`, `src/lib/orgs/hidden-org-access.ts:55-64`). (b) The video or club photo of a post that was removed, or whose author is suspended or banned (`src/app/api/posts/[id]/media/route.ts:94-105`). (c) Which account each waitlist entry became: the waitlist row shows the handle of the account that used the code, next to the name and answers (`src/lib/waitlist/admin-view.ts`, `joinedHandle`). The section 1 list has none of these.

3. **Deletion takes more from other people than the paragraph lists.** Other people's comments on your posts, with their likes, and other people's likes and reposts of your posts (`supabase/migrations/20260504130000_post_likes_comments.sql:6,17`, `20260508100000_post_reposts.sql:14`). Other people's replies under your comments (`20260508120000_comment_likes_and_replies.sql:44`). Everyone's votes and sign-ups on a poll or sign-up you posted in a club chat (`20261001110000_chat_v2_cards.sql:611-634`). Cancel notices you sent as an officer disappear from the people who got them (`20260930100000_event_cancel_notices.sql:41-42`).

4. **What a suspension or ban does not do, and one thing it costs.** Messages you already sent stay visible in every chat: the message rules hide removed messages, not a restricted sender's (`mod.sql:2232-2251`). Your club memberships and the events you made stay. A suspended or banned account can still read through the app's data requests: the proxy only blocks writes (`src/proxy.ts:229-246`). Your push devices are removed (`src/lib/moderation/actions.ts:256-271`). And Vibe+ keeps billing through a suspension while Settings and the Vibe+ page are closed to you; the notice tells you to write in (`src/app/account/suspended/page.tsx`, suspension paragraph; `src/proxy.ts:165-173`). The policy says only that a ban cancels Vibe+.

5. **A removed post takes other people's comments out of view.** When a moderator removes a post, every comment under it is hidden from everyone except the person who wrote that comment (`mod.sql:132-137`, `:2190-2196`). Section 5h speaks only to the author of the removed thing.

6. **The verify step answers two questions about someone else's school address, before any email is sent.** Any signed-in account that types a school address into the verify step is told "That school email is already linked to another account" if it is (`src/app/api/auth/school-email/request/route.ts:217-226`), and "This school email can't be used on Vibe" if that address is under a suspension or ban (`:232-235`; `src/lib/auth/school-email-apply.ts:51-58`, `:93-99`). Rate limits slow it (`:150`, `:165`), but one classmate's address can be tested: does this person have an account, is this person suspended or banned. Section 11 promises the waitlist form gives "the same answer for everyone"; this path does not. Fix in code if possible (one answer, refuse at the confirm step); spelling it out in the policy would hand out the method.

7. **One list that deletion does not reach.** `signup_allowlist` holds a full email address, a reason and a note for the few addresses allowed in without a code (`supabase/migrations/20261003100000_waitlist.sql:153-160`). Nothing removes the row when that account is deleted: the deletion trigger touches only `waitlist` (`20261003120000_waitlist_hardening.sql:477-502`). It belongs in "what stays" (it covers the store-review accounts, founders and hand exceptions).

8. **A suspension or ban is also written on the sign-in record at Supabase.** The kind and end date are copied into the account's sign-in data so the app can route you to the notice (`src/lib/moderation/actions.ts:191-215`). It goes with the account on deletion. Section 6 lists what Supabase holds and does not mention it.

9. **A club officer's removal shows no reason.** If a club owner, admin or mod removes your chat message you see "Removed by a club officer." and no reason (`src/lib/chat/message-read.ts:543-556`; `src/components/safety/ReportSheet.tsx:696-700`). Section 5h's "marked as removed with the reason" is about Vibe moderators only.
