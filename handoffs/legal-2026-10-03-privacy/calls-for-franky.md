## 3. What the policy still admits that a code fix would remove

- Deleting a message leaves its photo or video in storage.
- Group chat pictures, replaced work logos and unused uploads are never deleted.
- A comment has no delete button, and a join request cannot be taken back.
- Photos on club posts and in chats keep location details.
- In a club chat a block only hides messages on screen.
- Chat members may tell when you read or muted a chat.
- Any signed-in account can learn anyone's last-active time.
- Deleting an officer's account deletes the club's events and their chats.
- A failed server request can send sign-in details to Sentry.
