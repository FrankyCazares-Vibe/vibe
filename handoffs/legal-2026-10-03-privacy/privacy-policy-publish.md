# Vibe Privacy Policy

Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Vibe is a campus social app for people with an Indiana University or Purdue University email address. It is run by **CONNECTVIBE. LLC**, an Indiana limited liability company ("we", "us"). Vibe is independent. It is not run or backed by Indiana University, Purdue University, or any campus, school or club.

This page says what Vibe knows about you, who can see it and how to delete it. It covers connectvibe.app and the Vibe apps for iPhone and Android once they are in the stores.

**Only on the waitlist? Go to section 11. It is all you need.**

## The short version

- Most of your profile, including your posts, is open to anyone on the internet, and search engines can list it.
- Club pages are public too, with their 12 newest posts and who wrote them.
- When you are signed in, we record each profile you open and each post you open or pause on. You cannot turn this off. The owner sees the count and, with Vibe+ (a paid plan, not on sale yet), who.
- All three cofounders moderate Vibe, and a reported message is copied for them (section 1).
- One cofounder can reach everything Vibe stores. We read private messages only in the cases in section 5h.
- We do not sell your information. Vibe has no ads and no AI features, and we do not train AI on what you post.
- Photos and videos you send in chats or post as a club can still say where they were taken.
- You can delete your account in Settings, right away. Your messages vanish from other people's chats too. Sections 8 and 9 list what stays.
- "Verified" means the account could once read mail at an @iu.edu or @purdue.edu address. It does not prove enrollment or identity.
- Vibe is for people 18 or older. You confirm it with a checkbox.

## 1. Who we are and who can reach your data

Vibe is built and run by the three cofounders of CONNECTVIBE. LLC ("the Vibe team"). **All three moderate Vibe.** Their admin tools show them:

- every report, with the reporter's words and handle (their @name) and a copy of what was reported (section 5h);
- for any account: handle, name, verified or not, join date, any suspension or ban with its reason and private note, and the moderation actions about it;
- every club and its posts, hidden ones included;
- the photos an account, club or group chat shows, and briefly ones that were taken down, including a removed post's media;
- the waitlist (section 11).

They can look an account up by its exact email address, and the result does not show the address. The admin tools do not show email addresses (except on the waitlist), passwords, résumés, messages that were not reported, who viewed what, billing details or IP addresses (the number that identifies your internet connection).

**One cofounder also has direct access** to the systems that store Vibe's data and to our accounts at the companies in section 6. That access can read everything Vibe stores, including both your email addresses and every message. Vibe's own database keeps no record of what is read this way or in the admin tools, except that showing a full waitlist address is logged first.

## 2. Who can use Vibe, and what "verified" means

**Age and agreement.** You must be 18 or older. Sign-up has one checkbox: "I'm 18 or older and I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy." Checking it is how you agree to this policy. We do not ask for a date of birth or check age any other way.

**Making an account.** Sign-up asks for an email address, a password and the checkbox. While we let people in one group at a time (a wave), it also asks for the access code from your invite email. You then confirm your sign-in email, verify a school email and set up a profile.

**What "verified" means.** We send a link and a code to your school email and you use one. That proves you could read mail at that address at that moment. It does not prove that you are enrolled, employed or admitted, or that you are who your profile says. We do not contact the university. One school email verifies one account. Your campus is your own choice, and we do not check it.

**Without a verified school email** you cannot post, comment, message or join a club. You can still like, save, follow and report. Verifying hides nothing: an unverified account can read everything in section 5b, except résumés.

## 3. What we collect

### 3a. Your account

- **Sign-in email.** Shown to you in Settings. Other people are not shown it, apart from the starter name below.
- **Password.** We never store it. Supabase, our sign-in provider, stores a scrambled version that cannot be read back.
- **Starter name.** Until you choose a name, your display name is the part of your sign-in email before the @, and anyone who opens your profile can read it.
- **School email.** Stored once you confirm it. Other people never see it.
- **Proof that you agreed** (section 13) and, if you joined with an access code, a record of it (section 11).

### 3b. Your profile

Everything on your profile page (section 5a), plus up to three résumé or portfolio documents (a file or an outside link), the redaction bars you draw on them (black bars that cover parts of a résumé), the sections you hid, and your "What are you here for?" answers.

### 3c. What you post and do

- Posts, drafts, comments, likes, reposts and saves.
- Who you follow and who follows you.
- Clubs: memberships and roles, clubs you follow, requests to join with your message, invites, and, if a club officer removes you, who did it and when.
- Club events, your RSVPs (Going or Interested) and personal calendar entries.
- Messages and everything in them, your settings in each chat, and poll votes and sign-ups in club chats.
- Reports you file, and your block and mute lists.
- Otto settings and reminders. Otto, Vibe's built-in helper, runs on our own code, not an AI service.

**Photo details.** A camera saves hidden details inside a photo or video, such as where it was taken.

- **We remove them from:** your profile photo and cover, photos on your own posts, work logos and résumé images. Photos uploaded before late September 2026 may still carry theirs.
- **We do not remove them from:** videos, photos on a club's posts, club logos and banners, photos and videos sent in chats, group chat pictures, and résumé PDFs.

### 3d. What Vibe records by itself

- **Views.** When you are signed in and open a profile, open a post, or a post stays on your screen in the feed for about a second, we record who you are, what you viewed and the day, once per day. For a profile we also record the time of your first look that day. Your own views and signed-out views are not recorded.
- **Last active.** While the Campus page is open, we store the latest time your browser checked in.
- **Abuse limits.** We count how often some actions happen, mostly per account. A few counts hold an IP address or part of one. Nowhere else in Vibe's own tables holds one. Counts are cleared on no fixed schedule and can stay for weeks.
- **Email log.** We log each email our code sends: its kind, your account if there is one, the part of the address after the @, the time and whether it worked. In place of the address, the log holds a scrambled code. The code cannot be turned back into the address, but with a secret key held on our servers we can test whether a given address matches.
- **Error reports.** When something breaks, and for about one in ten page loads and server requests, a report goes to Sentry. Our code does not tag reports with your account or email, and strips sign-in links, access codes and profile handles from web addresses. A report can still include a club's handle, a post's number or search words. One about a failed server request can include the sign-in details your browser sent, which identify your account.
- **Search words** are not stored in our database, but our hosting company can log them as part of the web address.

### 3e. What we do not collect

- No tool that tracks what you tap, and no advertising tracker.
- No location from your device or your IP address. Some photos and videos you upload do carry a location inside the file (section 3c).
- No date of birth, phone number, contact list or card number, and no sign-in through Google, Apple or anyone else.

One automated check exists: a small set of words is blocked before text is saved, in messages too.

### 3f. Features that are switched off today

**Vibe+ (paid).** Once billing is on, you pay on Stripe's own pages and card numbers never reach us. We send Stripe only your Vibe account number, and we store your Stripe customer and subscription numbers, your Vibe+ status and a list of payment events. Vibe+ is not sold inside the iPhone or Android app.

**Notifications to your phone or browser.** If you turn them on, we store the address your device gives us until you sign out, turn them off or delete your account. A notification shows the sender's name and, with previews on, up to 100 characters of the message. Browser notifications travel encrypted through the browser maker's service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft). App notifications go through Google's Firebase and, on iPhone, Apple, which can read them. After your first yes in the app, Firebase contacts Google each time the app opens, even after you turn notifications off or sign out.

**The store apps.** The iPhone app asks for the camera, microphone and photo library only when you take, pick or save a photo or video, and for notifications when you turn them on. The Android app asks only for notifications. Neither has location, contacts, tracking or ad code.

### 3g. If you write to us

You need no account to email us a report, an intimate-image removal request or a child-safety tip. To recover an account we may ask for proof that it is yours. What you send stays in our support inbox, which the Vibe team reads, until one of us deletes it. We aim to delete recovery proof within 90 days after the request closes, though nothing does this automatically.

## 4. How we use it

We use your information to run Vibe, including suggesting people and clubs from who you follow and your major. We use it to keep accounts secure, to review reports and act on them, to fix bugs, and to answer you. We email you only to confirm your account, reset your password, verify your school email, confirm your waitlist spot and send your access code. There is no marketing or activity email.

We do not sell your personal information, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

## 5. Who can see what

### 5a. Anyone on the internet, with no account

- **Your profile page:** everything on it that you have not hidden, such as your name, handle, bio, major and year, campus, photos, work experience, the verified badge, and your follower and following counts. Also your published posts that were not made as a club. Not shown: either email address, résumés or "here for" answers.
- **Photos and videos:** profile photos, covers, logos, and the photos and videos on posts and club pages open for anyone who has the exact link. For most photos the link keeps working.
- **A post's share link:** the page asks for sign-in, but its preview shows the author's name, up to 140 characters of the text, and the photo.
- **Club pages:** name, description, links, logo, banner, who can join, member count, and follower count once it reaches 5. Also the 12 newest posts with each author's name, handle and photo, even for clubs you must ask or be invited to join.
- **Search engines and link previews:** profile and club pages can appear in search results and in link previews. We cannot pull back those copies.

Not public: your reposts, follower and following lists, chats, RSVP lists and member lists. A hidden club, or the profile of a suspended or banned account, looks like a page that does not exist. A block only works while the other person is signed in. Signed out, they still see your public profile and posts.

### 5b. Every signed-in account, verified or not, at every campus

- Everything above, plus your comments and reposts, and the lists of who you follow and who follows you.
- Totals of likes, views and saves on each post. The author gets a notification naming each liker. Nobody else sees who liked, and nobody sees who saved.
- Club events, with Going and Interested totals.
- Search finds verified accounts that finished setting up, across both universities. The Map lists people by campus and major, and the members of a club that anyone can join.
- For any account: the day it was made, roughly when it was last active, when its handle last changed and its total profile views.

### 5c. Résumés

Another person can open your résumé only if they are signed in with a verified school email, there is no block between you, you are not suspended or banned, and you have not hidden it. If you drew redaction bars, they get a copy with the bars made permanent, not the original. An outside link you give as a résumé or work logo loads from that site, which then sees the viewer's IP address.

### 5d. Who viewed you

You see how many people viewed your profile and each of your posts. With Vibe+ you also see who and when: profile viewers for the last 90 days, post viewers with no time limit. People on either side of a block, and people you muted, are left off the names but still counted. We can give Vibe+ to an account ourselves.

### 5e. People in your chats

- **Direct and group chats.** Members see every message, photo, video, shared post and reaction, who is in the chat, when the other side last read it and who is typing. Someone added later can read the whole history. Anyone in the chat can pin a message for everyone in it, and we store who pinned it and when. A message from someone you are not connected with (you do not follow each other) arrives as a request, and the sender sees when you read it.
- **Blocks.** A block stops new messages both ways, and a one-to-one chat leaves both inboxes. Nothing is deleted. In a shared group chat, old messages stay visible and neither of you can send while the block lasts.
- **Club chats.** Each club has a Board and a Lounge for every member, an Officers room for the club owner, club admins and people they add, and any rooms they create. Each event has a chat for club members who marked Going or Interested and for the club's owners and admins, and its member list names them. New members can read a room's whole history.
- **Polls and sign-ups.** With names on, the room sees who picked what, and sign-ups always show names. In an "Anonymous" poll no member or officer can see who voted. The vote is still stored with your account, so the direct access in section 1 could.
- **Blocks in club chats.** You each see a blank in place of the other's messages. This only hides them on screen, so treat what you write in a club room as readable by every member, including someone you blocked.
- **Your chat settings.** Other members of a chat may be able to tell when you last read it and whether you muted, pinned, hid or cleared it.

### 5f. Club owners, admins and mods

"Officers" means a club's owner, admins and mods.

- **Every member** sees the club's member list: name, handle, photo, role and join date.
- **Club owners and admins** see the follower list with the date each person first followed, invites and their outcome, and who RSVP'd to the club's events. They can remove a follower without that person being told, and delete any post made as the club.
- **All officers** see pending requests to join with your message, and a "Removed before" mark. They can remove a club chat message. It is then hidden from everyone but its author, not deleted, and we record who removed it.
- Officers are not shown email addresses or reports. If they try to remove a chat message that has an open report, the app tells them one is open.

### 5g. Only you

Drafts, saved posts, personal calendar entries, your notifications, your mute list, Otto settings and reminders, your redaction bars, your hidden-sections list and your "here for" answers. The person you block gets no notification, but if they open your profile while signed in, Vibe tells them the account has restricted them.

### 5h. What we at Vibe can see

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted (locked so that only the people in a chat can read them). The admin tools show a private message only when it was reported. The cofounder with direct access (section 1) could open any message. We read private messages only:

1. when one is reported to us;
2. when you ask us to, for example for a copy of your data;
3. when we have specific evidence that a conversation is being used for spam, threats, exploitation or another serious break of the Terms;
4. when the law requires it.

This is a rule we keep ourselves. No system enforces or records it.

**Reports.** When you report something, we keep a copy of it as it was: its text, links to its media, and the owner's name and handle. The person reported is not told about the report or who filed it, though they may guess. People on the Vibe team decide reports, not software. We aim to look at each within 24 hours.

**Removed content.** If the Vibe team removes your post, comment or message, you still see its text, marked as removed with the reason. A removed post's photo is taken offline for everyone, and the comments under it are hidden from everyone but their writers. Nobody else sees removed content, apart from the Vibe team.

### 5i. Universities, emergencies and the law

Vibe sends nothing to IU or Purdue automatically. Emails to your school email are delivered by the university's mail system, which can see them.

We hand over information when the law requires it, for example under a subpoena or court order. We may also go to the police, emergency services or the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) ourselves when we believe someone is in danger or a child is at risk.

If Vibe is ever sold or merged, we aim to tell you before your information moves to a new owner.

## 6. Outside services

These outside services hold your information or are contacted by your device. Each works under its own published terms and privacy policy.

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in, file storage). The whole database, your sign-in (email, scrambled password and a log of sign-in activity), public photos, private résumés and evidence files. Your browser talks to it directly when you sign in and each time a profile or post photo loads, so it sees your IP address and browser. It sends the "confirm your account" email.
- **Vercel** (hosting). It runs the site and our server code. Its visit logs record the web address requested, your IP address and browser. Some web addresses carry personal details, such as search words or the link in one of our emails.
- **Cloudflare** (media storage). Post videos, club media, chat photos and videos, and group pictures. It also serves the small program that shows a résumé PDF.
- **Resend** (sending email). The address and full content of every email our code sends.
- **Sentry** (finding bugs). Error reports and speed measurements (section 3d), with your IP address, browser and the page address.
- **Google** (fonts). Your IP address and browser on almost every page, and when a mail app loads the fonts in one of our emails.
- **Our email inbox.** Mail you send us is held by the email service that hosts our inbox.
- **AI coding tools.** We build and maintain Vibe with them. When the cofounder with direct access uses one to look into a problem, the database records it reads pass through that tool's provider.

Off today: **Stripe** and the notification services (section 3f), and the **Apple App Store and Google Play** once the apps are published.

Each company decides where its servers are. Ask and we will tell you what we know.

## 7. Cookies and what is stored on your device

**Cookies.** Only the ones that keep you signed in, set by our sign-in provider. They last up to 400 days and are refreshed as you use Vibe. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

**Kept in your browser.** To load faster, Vibe keeps copies of some of your own information in your browser, such as your profile card, your own posts, follow list and recent searches. They are cleared when you sign out or delete your account in that browser, and stay in your other browsers until you sign out there. Your theme, the email you just signed up with and a few similar notes can stay until you clear your browser data for Vibe.

**Sign out.** Signing out in Settings signs your account out on every device, though another device can keep working for up to about an hour.

## 8. How long we keep things, and what happens to files

Vibe does not delete things just because they are old. They stay until you, a club or we delete them. The one clean-up on a schedule today is the waitlist's (section 11).

| What | Kept until |
|---|---|
| Who viewed a profile or post | Either account or the post is deleted |
| Messages | The sender deletes the message or their account. In club chats, also when the post above it, the room, the event or the club is deleted |
| An event's chat | Deleted with the event. It closes to new messages 7 days after the event ends |
| RSVPs | You take it back, an officer removes you from the club, or the event, the club or your account is deleted |
| Events, join requests, invites | The event, the club or your account is deleted |

**Deleting one thing.**

- **A post** is deleted right away with its likes, comments, saves, reposts and view records. We then try to remove its files. If that fails, they stay until your account (or the club, for a club post) is deleted. If a report about the post is open, its files are kept as evidence. Temporary copies of a public photo can linger on our storage provider's network for a time we have not measured. You cannot delete or edit a post the Vibe team removed.
- **An edit** replaces the old text. A report filed before the edit keeps the text as it was.
- **A message** is deleted right away for everyone, with its reactions and, for a Board post, every reply under it.
- **A profile photo or cover** you replace is deleted, or kept as evidence if a report about you is open. A résumé file is deleted when you remove it.

**What Vibe does not delete yet.**

- The photo or video on a message you deleted. The app stops showing it, but the file stays in our storage, even after you delete your account. A link opened before the delete can work for up to an hour.
- Group chat pictures.
- A work logo you replaced, and a photo you uploaded but never used. They stay at their public links until you delete your account.
- Photos and videos you posted as a club, until the club is deleted.
- Comments. There is no delete button. A comment goes when its post or your account does.
- Your messages in a chat or club you have left.
- A direct or group chat itself. "Clear chat history" and "Delete conversation" only clear your view.
- A request to join a club. You cannot take it back.
- An account that never confirmed its email or never finished setting up. If you cannot sign in to delete it, write to help@connectvibe.app.
- Our email log, the abuse limit counts and emails you send us.
- Reports, the moderation log, suspension and ban records, and evidence files. No end date is set (section 9).

**Backups.** We keep no database backups, so we cannot bring back something you delete by mistake.

## 9. Your choices, and deleting your account

**See and change.** You can edit your profile and hide any section of it. Settings shows your sign-in and school emails. To change your sign-in email, write to help@connectvibe.app.

**Block, mute, report.** Block or mute someone from their profile. Any account that has accepted the Terms can report, verified or not, unless it is suspended or banned.

**A copy of your data.** Email hello@connectvibe.app from your sign-in address. There is no export tool, so one of us puts it together, which means opening your records, messages included. We aim to send it within 30 days.

**Delete your account.** In Settings, under Danger zone, type your handle to confirm. The account is deleted right away, with no waiting period and no undo. The same button is on the "verify your campus" screen and the suspended notice. If you cannot sign in, write to help@connectvibe.app or see connectvibe.app/legal/delete-account. One of us then deletes the account's records and removes its files.

What happens, in order:

1. Vibe+, if you have it, is cancelled with no refund.
2. Your files are erased, except evidence files (below) and the files section 8 says we miss.
3. Your sign-in is deleted, and with it every record tied to the account: both email addresses, your profile and proof of agreement, everything you posted, sent, liked, saved or voted on, your follows, club memberships, RSVPs, blocks and mutes, view records in both directions, your notifications, and your waitlist entry.

If step 1 or 2 fails, the account is not deleted and you can try again.

**What your deletion takes from other people.** Messages you sent disappear from other people's chats, with the replies under your Board posts and the votes on polls you posted. Other people's comments, likes and reposts on your posts go with the posts. Posts and events you made as a club are deleted, and each event takes everyone's RSVPs and its whole chat with it.

**What stays after deletion.**

- **Suspension and ban records,** for both: the kind, dates, reason, private note, and up to two scrambled codes (section 3d) made from your school and sign-in emails. The school-email code stops that address being verified on another account while the suspension or ban lasts.
- **Reports about you,** with the copy of what was reported. Reports you filed stay, with your name taken off.
- **The moderation log:** your old account number, your handle at the time and, for a photo takedown, the old photo's address.
- **Evidence files:** the photos and videos of a post the Vibe team removed or that has a report open or acted on, a photo, cover or logo the team took down, and your photo and cover if a report about you was open. Photos already kept as evidence stay even if the report was dismissed.
- **The files that deleting your account misses** (section 8).
- **Club records where you acted as an officer,** with your name removed. A club you owned stays, with no owner.
- **Our email log** (without the scrambled code or your account number), abuse limit counts until they are cleared and, once billing is on, Stripe's payment records and our list of payment events.
- **Logs at our providers:** Supabase's sign-in log (which can hold your email address and IP address), Resend's record of emails sent to you, Vercel's visit logs and Sentry's reports. We have not confirmed how long each is kept.
- **Your handle,** which is free for someone else right away.

**If we suspend or ban your account.** You can still sign in, read the notice and the legal pages, sign out and delete your account. Everyone else stops seeing your profile, posts and comments, but messages you already sent stay visible in chats. The notice gives the kind, the end date of a suspension and the reason, not the private note. To appeal, email help@connectvibe.app. A ban cancels Vibe+ with no refund.

**If you decline a new version of the Terms,** you cannot post, comment, message or change your profile. Settings stays open, so you can still delete your account.

## 10. Security

**What we do.** All traffic uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Résumés, chat media and group pictures are served through links that expire. No other user can read your email addresses (apart from the starter name in section 3a), your proof of agreement or your moderation records.

**What we do not have.** Sign-in is by email and password, with no second sign-in step such as a code on your phone. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted (section 5h).

**If we learn of a breach** that exposes your personal information, we will email the people affected and say what happened, what information was involved and what we are doing about it, as fast as we reasonably can and within the time Indiana law requires.

## 11. The waitlist

Joining the waitlist does not make an account, and it is not agreeing to this policy. This section is our notice to you.

**What we collect.** From the form: your school email (IU or Purdue only) and your name. If you give them: your year, major, the clubs you are in, whether you run a club and which one, and one line on what you would want from Vibe. We also store:

- your university, from the address;
- where you found us, if your link carried a tag saying so;
- when you sent the form with each box checked: "I'm 18 or older and a current student", "Email me when it's my turn", and the optional "Also send me Vibe news";
- the state of your entry: when we emailed you, when you confirmed, your wave (we let people in one group at a time), and when a code was sent and by which cofounder;
- a log of each email we send you (its kind, the part of the address after the @, the time, whether it was sent), with a scrambled code in place of your address. The code cannot be turned back into the address.

The entry stores no IP address (the number that identifies your internet connection), browser, device or location, and the waitlist pages set no cookie. To stop abuse they count requests by IP address, and those counts can stay for weeks.

**Why.** To confirm your spot, choose who gets in each wave and email you your access code. No news email exists. If we ever send one, it is meant only for people who checked the news box. That checkbox is deleted with your entry, so we would have to ask you again.

**If a cofounder adds you** because you asked us, you checked no boxes. We store the address you gave us, your name, your clubs, your wave and who added you. Your first email from us is the access code. The same 40 days apply.

**Who handles it.** Supabase stores it, Vercel runs the site and the daily clean-up, and Resend sends our emails. Fonts load from Google and error reports go to Sentry. Vercel's visit logs see the code in our email links. Emails to a school mailbox are carried by your university's mail system. We send the universities nothing else.

**Who at Vibe sees it.** The three cofounders see the list with addresses partly hidden (the first letter and the part after the @), next to your name and answers and, once you sign up, your handle. A cofounder we have set up to do it can see a full address, one person at a time, and each look is logged first. A reply to one of our emails lands in our support inbox with your full address, outside this list and outside the 40 days. The form gives everyone the same answer, so it does not show who is on the list.

**How long.**

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| Your answers | Up to 40 days after you join the list |
| An entry you never confirm | Up to 30 days after its last confirm email |
| The confirm link | Works for 7 days |
| An access code | Works once, for up to 14 days |

The 40 days hold whatever state your entry is in. A daily clean-up deletes the whole entry. Sending the form again before you confirm starts the 40 days again, with your new answers.

**Leaving sooner.** Every waitlist email has a "Take me off the list" link. Open it, tap "Yes, take me off", and your entry is deleted right away. Use our newest email, because an older link may no longer work. With no email from us, write to hello@connectvibe.app from that address. After you make an account, the entry goes with the clean-up or when you delete the account.

**What stays after an entry is deleted.** Our email log, without the scrambled code. Resend's own record of the emails it sent, with your address and, in the access-code email, your name and the code. We have not confirmed how long Resend keeps it. Our log of cofounder actions, with no name or address.

**When you sign up with a code.** The email you sign up with is stored on your waitlist entry, linked to your new account, until the entry is deleted. Your account keeps a record of the code and a wave note (the wave, how you came, the day the code was sent and the day you joined), deleted with the account. Your waitlist answers are not copied into your profile. A few addresses we list ourselves can sign up without a code. That list keeps the address and a reason, even after the account is deleted.

**Questions,** or want a copy of your entry? Write to hello@connectvibe.app.

## 12. Age

If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and take its content down. We then aim to delete its information ourselves, except what the law requires us to keep.
