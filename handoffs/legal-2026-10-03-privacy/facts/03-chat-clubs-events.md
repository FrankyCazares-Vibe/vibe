How to read this file
- Paths are relative to the repo root. "mig" = `supabase/migrations/`. "mig A" = `20261001100000_chat_v2_rooms.sql`, "mig B" = `20261001110000_chat_v2_cards.sql`.
- "msg route" = `src/app/api/me/threads/[id]/messages/route.ts`. "del route" = `src/app/api/me/threads/[id]/messages/[messageId]/route.ts`.
- I read code, migrations and config only. No database query, no network call.
- LIVE NOW vs LANDING-NIGHT ONLY: nothing in this area differs between the two trees. `git diff --stat main origin/landing-night` over `src/app/api/me/threads`, `src/app/api/orgs`, `src/app/api/events`, `src/lib/chat`, `src/lib/orgs`, `src/lib/events`, `src/lib/push`, `src/components/mobile/messages`, `public/html/messages.html`, `src/app/legal` and the `20261001*` migrations prints nothing. Every fact below is true on both trees. The one landing-night item that touches clubs is the waitlist form (F47).
- "data API" = the Supabase REST endpoint a signed-in browser can call directly with its own login. Where the app's screens show less than the data API allows, I say both.
- Flags in production today (from the handoffs, not from code): `CHAT_V2_ENABLED` on for the three founders and one test club, everyone on Oct 26; `PUSH_ENABLED` off; billing off.
- WHAT THE CHAT FLAG GATES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT. Gated (404 with the flag off): the new phone inbox (`src/app/api/me/inbox/route.ts`), the club space (`src/app/api/orgs/[slug]/space/route.ts`), creating a card and voting (`.../threads/[id]/cards/route.ts`, `.../cards/[messageId]/vote/route.ts`). The calendar route (`src/app/api/me/calendar/route.ts:60-63, 116`) still answers with the flag off; only its three room fields come back empty. Those, plus `src/app/api/me/features/route.ts` (which only tells the screen whether the flag is on), are every route `grep -rln "chatV2For" src` finds. NOT gated: the rooms themselves and their rules (they are in the database for every club; mig B:110-112 records mig A as live on production since Sept 30), reading and sending in a room, Board replies, the officer "Remove", and "who's going" in an event chat (msg route and del route never call `chatV2For`).

## 2. Who can see it

- F48. ANYONE ON THE INTERNET (no account). Nothing from any chat, no RSVP list, no member list, no follower list. A club's page is public: name, description, links, logo, banner, member count (shown even when it is 1), follower count only from 5 up, who can join, and its recent posts with the author's name, handle and photo. A club our moderators hid answers "not found" to everyone outside it. The events block on a club page loads from a route that needs a login. - F49. ANY SIGNED-IN USER. Club events at the campus being browsed: title, description, place, times, the club, Going and Interested COUNTS, and their own RSVP. Through the data API (not through any app screen): the member list of a club that is open to join, public and not hidden. The app's own roster screen answers "Members only" to a non-member of any club. Evidence: `src/app/api/events/route.ts:54-60, 332-357`; mig `20260916103000:778-788`; `src/app/api/orgs/[slug]/members/route.ts:8-12, 78-82`.
- F50. PEOPLE IN A DM OR GROUP CHAT. Every message, photo, video, shared post and reaction in it; who is in it; in a DM or group, when the other side last read and who is typing now. Someone holding a pending request can read the messages before accepting. A block stops NEW messages both ways; it does not hide old ones in a DM or group ("blocks aren't a read filter in the thread view"). Evidence: mig `20260923090000:2232-2241`; msg route:177-226, 426-446; `.../messages/[messageId]/media/route.ts:49-51`.
- F51. OTHER MEMBERS OF THE SAME CHAT, THROUGH THE DATA API ONLY. The read rule on `channel_members` is "you are in this chat and may read its roster", and it returns whole rows. So a member can read each other member's read marker, mute-until, hide time, pin time, clear-history time, typing time and request state for that chat. No app screen shows these. Since mig A every club member holds a row on the club's Board and Lounge, so this now covers a whole club. The migrations say the column fix is "its own file" and it is not written yet. Evidence: mig `20260928150000:45-47, 79-81, 283`; mig A:59-61; `grep -n "channel_members" supabase/migrations/*.sql | grep -i "revoke select\|grant select ("` returns nothing.
- F52. MEMBERS OF A CLUB. The roster in the app: each member's name, handle, photo, verified badge, role and join date. Board and Lounge messages. An extra room when it is open, or when they are on its allow-list. The Officers room: owners, admins and people on its allow-list only (a mod is not in it by role). A new member can read everything already in the rooms they can enter: there is no join-date cut-off in the read rule. Evidence: `src/app/api/orgs/[slug]/members/route.ts:85-112`; mig A:1325-1340; mig `20260923090000:2244-2252`.

"Who's going": the Going and Interested counts, and the name, handle and photo of the first 8 people marked Going WHO ARE IN THE ROOM (club members), oldest RSVP first. The phone shows 5. People in a block pair with you, and accounts you cannot read, are skipped. This is the one place a member who is not an officer sees names of people going. It is read by the server with the service role and sent with every thread read of that room; that read is not behind the `chat_v2` flag. Evidence: `src/lib/chat/message-read.ts:274, 992-1044, 1106-1123`; msg route:141-174; `src/components/mobile/messages/club/EventAbout.tsx:18, 111`.

## 3. How long it is kept and what deletes it

There is no scheduled clean-up for anything in this area. `vercel.json` does not exist on main; on landing-night it holds one job, the waitlist purge. Everything below is deleted by an action, never by age.

- F61. MESSAGES are kept until: the sender deletes the message; the sender's account is deleted (everyone in the chat loses those messages: `messages.user_id ... ON DELETE CASCADE`, mig `20260430190000:116`); or the chat itself is deleted (F68, F69). There is no edit: `UPDATE` on messages is revoked from clients (mig `20260903110000:19`).
- F62. DELETING ONE MESSAGE (sender only, any chat). The row is hard-deleted at once, for everyone. With it go its reactions, a card's poll, options, votes and note, the mention notifications that point at it, and, for a Board post, every reply under it by anyone. Quotes of it keep their own text and lose the quote. del route:31-37, 96-109; mig A:713-714; mig B:611, 624, 633, 642; mig `20260515100000:24-26`.
- F63. THE ATTACHED FILE IS NOT DELETED with the message. The del route never calls R2. Account deletion later finds chat files by reading the user's remaining `messages.media_url` rows, so a file whose message was deleted first stays in R2 with nothing pointing at it. `src/lib/r2.ts:311-327`; `src/lib/profile/storage-purge.ts:44-55, 198-223, 375`.
- F64. "CLEAR CHAT HISTORY" deletes nothing. It stamps `cleared_at` on your row; the app then hides older messages and their media from you only. The other side keeps everything and the rows stay in the database. Words: "Clear chat history? This only clears your view; the other side keeps their copy." In a club room the stamp does nothing: the read ignores it. `.../threads/[id]/clear/route.ts:7-15, 27-31`; `src/components/mobile/messages/ConversationActionSheet.tsx:130`; `src/lib/chat/message-read.ts:850`.
- F65. "DELETE CONVERSATION" deletes nothing. It stamps `hidden_at`; the chat comes back when a new message arrives or you open it again. Words: "Delete this conversation? It'll hide from your view, but the other side will still see it." `.../threads/[id]/hide/route.ts:7-11`; `ConversationActionSheet.tsx:155`; `src/app/api/me/threads/route.ts:356-369`.
- F66. DECLINING A REQUEST deletes only your own pending membership row. The sender keeps the chat and the messages. LEAVING A GROUP (or being removed by its admin) deletes the membership row; the messages you sent stay for the others. A club room cannot be left: "You can't leave a club's chat. Leave the club instead." `.../decline/route.ts:9-16, 56-62`; `.../members/[userId]/route.ts:10-22, 108-112`; `src/lib/chat/types.ts:129`.
- F67. A DM or group nobody is left in is not cleaned up. The only code that deletes a `dm` or `group` chat is the roll-back of a failed create (`src/app/api/me/threads/route.ts:331, 396`). Its messages go only as each sender's account is deleted. The group's photo in R2 is never deleted by any code path (NOT FOUND: no `groups/` delete in `src/lib/r2.ts` or `storage-purge.ts`).
- F68. REMOVED MESSAGES (by a club officer or by a Vibe moderator) stay in the database, hidden from everyone but the author, until the author deletes the message (allowed: the delete rule is only "it is yours"), the author's account is deleted, or the room goes. The `chat_removals` record goes with the message; if the remover deletes their account the record stays with the remover blank. A report's snapshot of the text is kept separately (see the moderation facts file). mig `20260509100000:16-19`; mig `20260923090000:201-210`; mig B:650-655.
- F69. CLUB ROOMS. Board, Lounge and Officers live as long as the club. An extra room can be deleted by an owner or admin, with all its messages. AN EVENT'S CHAT, with every message in it from everyone, is deleted when the event row is deleted: an officer cancels or deletes the event; the club is deleted; OR THE ACCOUNT THAT CREATED THE EVENT IS DELETED (`events.creator_id ... ON DELETE CASCADE`, then `channels.event_id ... ON DELETE CASCADE`). It is not deleted 7 days after the event; it only stops taking messages then (F18). `src/app/api/orgs/[slug]/channels/[channelId]/route.ts:203-229`; mig A:710, 729-730; mig `20260430190000:124`; mig `20260928110000:111` (live state: `events_creator_id_fkey ON DELETE CASCADE`).
- F70. POLLS, SIGN-UPS AND VOTES. A card cannot be edited. It goes when its message goes (F62). You can take a vote or a sign-up spot back at any time while the card is open. Your votes in a room are deleted when you lose that room: you leave the club, are removed, are demoted out of an event chat, or take your RSVP back. Owners and admins keep theirs. Known gap written in the migration: votes in the Officers room survive leaving. Your votes also go with your account. mig B:832-833, 886-888, 932-973, 634.
- F71. RSVPs are kept until: you remove yours (always allowed, even with no Terms accepted or after a campus move); the event is deleted; an officer REMOVES you from the club (your RSVPs to that club's events that have not ended are deleted; leaving on your own keeps them); or your account is deleted. An RSVP to an event that already happened stays. `src/app/api/events/[id]/rsvp/route.ts:185-226`; mig `20260928100000:51-57`; mig `20260430190000:134-141`.
- F72. EVENTS are kept until an officer cancels or deletes them, the club is deleted, or the account that created them is deleted. Ended events are not deleted; the campus list just stops showing them (`ends_at >= now()`). "Cancel" and "Delete" are the same call: the row is gone, with its RSVPs and its chat. If it had not ended, everyone who was Going or Interested gets a notice "from the club"; that notice keeps its own copy of the title and start time, and the officer's id as actor (never shown). `src/app/api/events/[id]/route.ts:129-153`; `src/app/api/events/route.ts:102-103`; mig `20260928110000:64-68`; mig `20260930100000:4-9, 24-27`.
- F73. PERSONAL CALENDAR ENTRIES: until you delete them or your account. mig `20260508130000:15`; `src/app/api/me/personal-events/[id]/route.ts`.
- F74. CLUB MEMBERSHIP: until you leave, an officer removes you, or your account or the club is deleted. Leaving or removal deletes your room rows, your allow-list rows and your votes. THE MESSAGES YOU WROTE IN THE CLUB'S ROOMS STAY and stay readable to members. `src/app/api/orgs/[slug]/join/route.ts:258-271`; mig `20260928100000:48-57`.
- F75. FOLLOWING A CLUB: until you unfollow, an owner or admin removes you, you leave a club whose follow was made by joining it, or your account or the club is deleted. The first-follow date outlives unfollowing and removal; it is deleted only with your account or the club. mig `20260916120000:636-650, 678-680`; `.../followers/[userId]/route.ts:24-28`.
- F76. JOIN REQUESTS: the row and the message you wrote stay after the request is approved or denied. NOT FOUND: any way for the student to withdraw a pending request or delete its message (`grep -rn "org_join_requests" src | grep -i "delete\|withdraw\|cancel"` returns nothing; `requests/[id]` has only the officer's POST). Deleted with your account or the club. If the officer who answered deletes their account, "answered by" goes blank. mig `20260507100000:55-70`.
- F77. INVITES: an unanswered invite expires after 30 days (marked expired the next time something touches it; there is no job). The row and its outcome stay until the invited person's account or the club is deleted. - F79. NOTIFICATIONS from this area have no age limit. A mention row goes when its message is deleted or either account is deleted. An event-cancelled row goes when the club is deleted or the officer who cancelled deletes their account (the person who was notified then loses the notice). mig `20260515100000:24-26`; mig `20260930100000:30-33`.
- F80. ACCOUNT DELETION, this area only (the account facts file has the full flow).

Foreign keys in mig `20260430190000:104-141`, mig B:618, 634, mig `20260916120000:641-664`, mig `20260928100000:240-244`; `src/lib/profile/storage-purge.ts:44-55`.

In a small room the counts plus "which one is mine" can also give a vote away.
- S2. DELETING AN ACCOUNT DELETES A CLUB'S EVENTS. Every event that account created goes, with everyone's RSVPs and the whole event chat, other people's messages included (F69). No cancel notice is sent on that path: the notice is written only by the cancel route (`src/app/api/events/[id]/route.ts:136-148`). An officer who graduates and deletes their account empties the club's calendar.
- S3. DELETING A BOARD POST DELETES OTHER PEOPLE'S REPLIES under it, and so does the poster's account deletion (F62; mig A:713, 733-734).
- S4. THE OTHER MEMBERS OF A CHAT CAN READ YOUR READ MARKER, MUTE, HIDE AND CLEAR STAMPS through the data API (F51). No screen shows them. Since the rooms migration this spans every member of a club.
- S5. FILES OUTLIVE THEIR MESSAGES. Deleting a message leaves its photo or video in storage with nothing pointing at it, and account deletion cannot find it (F63). Group photos are never deleted (F67). There is no clean-up job.
- S6. CHAT PHOTOS KEEP LOCATION METADATA (F4) and are served through a signed link that works for an hour for anyone holding it (F82).
- S7. TWO BUTTONS THAT SAY MORE THAN THEY DO. "Delete conversation" only hides it; "Clear chat history" only hides it from you. The confirm text is honest; the labels are not (F64, F65).
- S8. A JOIN-REQUEST MESSAGE CANNOT BE TAKEN BACK. No route withdraws a pending request or deletes its text; it stays after the decision; owners, admins and mods can read it (F40, F76).
- S9. AFTER YOU LEAVE A CLUB OR A GROUP, YOUR MESSAGES STAY AND THE APP NO LONGER LETS YOU DELETE THEM. The delete route first reads the message as you; you can no longer read the room, so it answers 403 "Not a member" (del route:77-116; `src/lib/messages/channel-access.ts:94-99` "UNREADABLE CHANNEL = NOT A MEMBER"). Whether a direct data-API delete still works for an ex-member: NOT TESTED (no database access in this audit). Account deletion does remove them.
- S10. RSVP NEEDS NO VERIFIED SCHOOL EMAIL. The route checks sign-in, the rate limit, Terms, the hidden-club rule and the home-campus rule; the two database rules check "your own row", not restricted, home campus, club visible. None asks for school verification (`src/app/api/events/[id]/rsvp/route.ts:147-165`; mig `20260928110000:28-44, 99-102`). The Terms v2 (:28) say "the campus pages ask you to verify before they open", so in practice an unverified account would need an event's id; the Terms list RSVP among the things a bare login may do (T6).
- S11. BLOCKS IN CLUB ROOMS ARE DONE BY THE APP, NOT THE DATABASE. A direct data-API read of a club room still returns a blocked person's messages (F60). In a DM a block leaves the old conversation readable to both.
- S12. "WHO'S GOING" NAMES PEOPLE TO OTHER MEMBERS. The simple rule "only organizers see who RSVP'd" has this one exception (F53).
- S13. NEW MEMBERS READ THE WHOLE PAST of the Board and the Lounge (F52).
- S14. THE REMOVAL RECORD IS WRITTEN AND NEVER SHOWN. `chat_removals` says which officer removed a message; no screen reads it (F23). The author is told "by a club officer", never who.
- S15. THE AUTHOR OF A MESSAGE A VIBE MODERATOR REMOVED CAN READ THAT MODERATOR'S ACCOUNT ID through the data API (F24).
- S16. THE MEMBER COUNT IS PUBLIC AT ANY SIZE, while the follower count is hidden below 5 "because the number names people" (F48; `src/lib/orgs/following.ts:85-88`).
- S17. WHICH OFFICER CREATED A CLUB EVENT is in every event row any signed-in user can read through the data API (`events.creator_id`). The app's own list sends that id with no name or handle, on purpose (`src/app/api/events/route.ts:25-33`); the id can be looked up.
- S18. A CANCEL NOTICE DISAPPEARS IF THE OFFICER WHO CANCELLED DELETES THEIR ACCOUNT (F79).
- S19. A CLUB-LEVEL REMOVAL IS FORGOTTEN ON RE-SIGN-UP (F80, side effect).
- S20. NOTHING HERE EVER EXPIRES BY AGE (section 3). Past events, old RSVPs, answered requests, resolved invites and notifications stay until an account or a club is deleted.
- S21. BEFORE `PUSH_ENABLED` FLIPS the policy has to say that message text can appear on a lock screen and passes through a push service (F45, F84).
