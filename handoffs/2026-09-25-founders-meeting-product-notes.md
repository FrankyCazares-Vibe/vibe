# Founders' meeting: product asks (from Franky's notes, recorded 2026-09-25)

Marketing items from the same meeting live in `~/vibe-marketing/research/founder-questions.md`.

## Build list (not yet sequenced; pick order with Franky)

1. **Club and org roles and ownership**
   - Show a club's member list on its profile.
   - Members can be given roles (admin, moderator, and so on) by whoever has the right to do that.
   - Ownership can be transferred or passed down, so a club survives when its officers graduate.
   - Check against the S61 org model (following ≠ membership, invite-only join, officers) before designing. Decide who can grant which role and whether a transfer needs the new owner to accept.
2. **Club badges on profiles.** Joining a club or org puts its badge on your profile. Open questions: does leaving remove it, can users hide it, and what about private or hidden orgs?
3. **One slim rolling strip.** Remove the separate Heads up, Announcements and RSVPs blocks and merge them into one slimmer rolling strip on phone and desktop. Heads up currently = the viewer's RSVP'd or saved events (see the Otto campus panel data sources in memory).
4. **Profile customization**
   - move widgets around
   - move the bio to the side
   - hide or remove widgets
   - The roadmap had movable widgets in November, with moving and hiding free. This meeting pulls the idea forward; confirm timing against the Oct 31 launch.
5. **Nearby campuses (view only).** See events and activity at other campuses within a set distance, but no chatting across them. This changes the S60 rule that campus things are limited to your own university; reconcile it with that rule rather than overriding it silently.
6. **Butler (implied by marketing).** The founders plan a "vibeButler" account. Butler University would need butler.edu verification and its own campus entry, a new school system beyond IU and Purdue.
