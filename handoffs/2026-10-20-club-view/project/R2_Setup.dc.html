Later, a club on its own.

Profile › your name › Start a club, on the phone or a laptop: name, photo, one line. Then “Get checked”: a code goes to the club’s email, or they ask Vibe. Until then the page is live, but events and invites wait, and the setup list says why.

Why not sign in with the club’s email?

A shared password is the Instagram mess we promise to fix: when officers graduate, the login leaves with them. Vibe also checks each person’s school email, and a club inbox isn’t a student. The club’s email still helps twice: it proves the club is real, and it gets the club back if its owner disappears.
