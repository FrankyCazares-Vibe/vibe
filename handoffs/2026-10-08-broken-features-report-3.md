## Executive Summary

Vibe is usable today. Nothing on this list crashes the app, loses a post or a message, or shows one student's private things to another. The worst single thing a tester can hit is a locked door at sign-up: a student whose IU address is already attached to an older account signs up again with that address, types the code, and then every page sends them to "Verify your campus," which refuses the very email they just used. They cannot get in and there is nothing on the screen to try next. A brand-new student with a fresh school email does not hit this, so most of the pilot walks past it, but any of the existing accounts could.

The pattern is simple: when Vibe says no, the screen does not say so. A student taps Going on an event, Follow on a classmate, a heart on a message, Unblock in Settings, or "8 hours" in the Mute sheet, the button lights up, and a second later it quietly goes back to how it was, or just sits there. The app had a reason each time, but the student only sees a button that will not stay pressed. That is five of the twenty items. A second cluster is the phone and the laptop drifting apart: the laptop has a Message button on a friend's profile but is missing the "This person is under 18" reason when you report someone, which the privacy page promises; the phone has that reason but no Message button. The third is loose ends from last week's switch to school-email-first sign-up: the login page still talks about a link that no longer exists, the code screen still asks for a "personal email," and a verification stop forgets where the student was headed and drops them on the setup screens instead of the chat they tapped.

Three of these cost real trust. First, on a phone there is no way to message someone from their profile, and the "Connected" button, the obvious thing to tap, unfollows them instead. A student concludes they just lost a friend and cannot even DM them; that is the kind of thing that gets screenshotted into a group chat before the app is closed. Second, sharing does not look like sharing. A post or club page pasted into iMessage, Discord, or an Instagram DM previews as the generic "Vibe · Your campus. Your people. Yourself." card, not the post or the club's name, and an old or mistyped link lands on a black page that only says the page could not be found, with no way into the app. Sharing is how a campus app spreads, and right now every share looks like an ad for Vibe instead of a friend's post. Third, the sign-up lock above, because a student who cannot get in on the first try does not make a second.

Here is what is not broken. The landing page, login, and sign-up with a school email for a new student all work, and the code arrives. Public profile and club pages load and show their content, even for someone who has never had an account; two spots show a stray "You've been signed out" note, but the page is there. The post and club links themselves still work; it is only the preview that is wrong. Reactions, RSVPs, follows, and unblocks all work in the normal case; the silent flip-back only happens on the rare occasion the app has refused. Nothing on the list touches what students can see of each other.

What this run could not check is everything behind login, and that is most of the app. No signed-in session was available, so the feed, posting, photos, club chat and direct messages in use, Settings, the campus map, and the new small Messages window in Otto's panel were never seen live. Three items are flagged for exactly that reason and should be the first things a founder walks: Otto's Messages tab on a laptop after last week's four rewrites, the campus map's club bubbles (there is a real chance a twenty-member club shows as empty), and push notifications. On push, the live settings this run could see have none of the six entries push needs, so it is likely that no phone buzzes for anyone, founders included, and that the Notifications section is missing from Settings; that one is uncertain and needs five minutes with a phone to confirm. A walk on a real phone, signed in as a normal student rather than an admin, is still the single most valuable check and it has not been done.

## Degraded — it works, but gives the wrong result

### You sign up with your IU email, type the code, and still can't get in

**In plain terms** — A student who already had a Vibe account under a personal email, with their school address verified on it, and then signs up again using that school address directly gets stuck: they confirm the code, but every page sends them to a "Verify your campus" screen that asks for a school email "that isn't the one you signed up with," and typing the school address there is refused as "already linked to another account." There is no way through, so the student concludes Vibe is broken and leaves. One real account is stuck this way right now, and nothing stops the next person from landing in the same place.

**What breaks** — "I signed up with my IU email and typed the code, but every page sends me to 'Verify your campus'. It tells me to enter a school email that isn't the one I signed up with, and when I enter my IU email anyway it says that email is already linked to another account. I can't get in."

**Why it happens** — `src/lib/auth/campus-access.ts:48-50` redirects to `/auth/school-email` whenever `!row.school_verified` and `verifySchoolFromSignInEmail(user)` is false. `src/lib/auth/school-sign-in.ts:42-47` returns false (a `console.error` only) on any non-200 from `applySchoolEmailVerification()`. `src/lib/auth/school-email-apply.ts:176-213` answers 409 `school_email_taken` when `schoolIdentityConflict()` or the exact `school_email` lookup finds another account holding the address as its verified `school_email`. The only escape, the manual screen (`src/app/auth/school-email/page.tsx:520-525`, which renders "not the one you signed up with"), submits to `src/app/api/auth/school-email/request`, which runs the same conflict check. The S75 sign-in-email path collides with the S64 one-address-per-account rule and nothing resolves the collision.

**Fix** — Treat a confirmed sign-in address as stronger proof than an old secondary. (1) In `applySchoolEmailVerification`, or a wrapper used only by `verifySchoolFromSignInEmail`, when the conflict is with the caller's own confirmed sign-in email, move the `school_email` to the new account and clear it on the old one instead of answering 409. (2) At sign-up, before `supabase.auth.signUp`, ask a small rate-limited server route (service role, answers yes/no only) whether the typed school address is already verified on an account, and say "You already have a Vibe account with this email, sign in" instead of creating a second account. (3) Where `verifySchoolFromSignInEmail` fails with `school_email_taken`, show that state on `/auth/school-email` with a sign-in link rather than the generic "enter a school email" form. Then unstick the one affected row by hand and walk the flow live with it.

**Where** — Counts-only SQL on production: `auth.users` with an @iu.edu/@purdue.edu sign-in email = 1; that account has `email_confirmed_at` set AND `public.users.school_verified = false` (1); its address equals a different account's verified `school_email` (`collides_with_legacy_secondary = 1`); created 2026-09, `ever_signed_in` 0. Code: `src/lib/auth/campus-access.ts:48-50`; `src/lib/auth/school-sign-in.ts:42-47`; `src/lib/auth/school-email-apply.ts:176-213` (409 `school_email_taken`); `src/app/auth/school-email/page.tsx:520-525`; `src/app/api/auth/school-email/request/route.ts` (same conflict check). Introduced by 7db8507 "feat(auth): school email first" (2026-10-08), merged in 63ac6a0, deployed in 33ce032; the stuck row predates S75 but is held by today's code. The dead end is proven from counts and code; the screens were not walked live with that account, since it has never signed in.

### On a phone, tapping "Connected" on a friend's profile unfollows them, and there is no way to message

**In plain terms** — On a phone, opening a friend's profile shows one button, "Connected," and no way to send them a message. Tapping that button, which looks like the natural way to start a chat, silently drops the connection instead, with no confirmation and no notice. On a laptop the same spot shows a "Message" button that opens the chat, so students who use both notice the phone is missing it, and the ones who tap "Connected" lose the connection without knowing.

**What breaks** — "On my phone I open a friend's profile and there's no way to message them. I tapped the 'Connected' button thinking it would open a chat and it unfollowed them instead. On my laptop that same button says Message."

**Why it happens** — `src/components/mobile/ProfileMobile.tsx:5600-5608`: the single visitor button's label is "Connected" / "Following" / "Follow back" / "Connect" and `onClick={onTap}` is the follow toggle. `toggleFollow` at `:745-757` sets `wasFollowing = following || connected` and sends `method: wasFollowing ? "DELETE" : "POST"` to `/api/me/follow`, so a tap on "Connected" unfollows. `grep -n "/messages" src/components/mobile/ProfileMobile.tsx` returns nothing. The desktop static profile (`public/html/profile.html:6768-6769`, `:6904`) has the `state-message` button that navigates to Messages.

**Fix** — Give the phone visitor profile a Message action in the connected state (a secondary button or a row in the profile's action sheet) that navigates to `/messages?to=<handle>`, matching the desktop button. Keep unfollow behind a confirm or inside the sheet so the primary "Connected" tap never silently drops the connection.

**Where** — Code: `src/components/mobile/ProfileMobile.tsx:5600-5608` (labels, no Message), `:745-757` (DELETE on connected), zero `/messages` matches in the file; `public/html/profile.html:6769` and `:6904`. History: `git log -S state-message -- public/html/profile.html` goes back to b69b67aa (the session-26 snapshot); `git log -S "/messages?to" -- src/components/mobile` finds nothing. The desktop Message button has existed since the session-26 prototype; the mobile profile rebuild never carried it. Code is unambiguous; a tap on a real phone would confirm the unfollow but was not performed.

### A link to your post, pasted into a group chat, shows the generic Vibe card instead of your post

**In plain terms** — When a student copies a link to their post and pastes it into iMessage, Discord, Slack or an Instagram DM, the preview that appears is the generic Vibe card ("Your campus. Your people. Yourself."), not their post's caption or picture. The chat app builds that preview while not signed in, and for anyone not signed in the link sends you to the login screen before it can show anything, so the preview is of the login screen. The post itself still opens after signing in; what the student loses is the moment where a friend sees their post in the chat and taps it, which is how Vibe spreads from one student to the next.

**What breaks** — "I pasted a link to my post in the group chat and the preview is just the generic Vibe card, not my post."

**Why it happens** — `src/app/posts/[id]/page.tsx:157-164` calls `redirect()` for `!user` on every signed-out request, so the HTTP answer is a 307 with no OG body. Unfurlers follow it to `/auth/login`, which inherits `src/app/layout.tsx:28-39` `openGraph.title` / `twitter.title`. The per-post `generateMetadata` tags never reach a crawler.

**Fix** — For a signed-out request, return a 200 shell that carries the `generateMetadata` tags plus a "Sign in to see this post" button (and a client-side redirect to login) instead of `redirect()`. Keep the existence read out of that branch so the 404-vs-redirect oracle stays closed. Also give `GENERIC_POST_METADATA` its own `openGraph` and `twitter` titles so the layout's explicit values do not win the merge.

**Where** — Live: `GET https://www.connectvibe.app/posts/00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000000` (anon) → 307, `Location: /auth/login?next=%2Fposts%2F…`; the 307 body has `<title>Post · Vibe</title>` but `<meta property="og:title" content="Vibe · Your campus. Your people. Yourself.">`; `GET /auth/login` → `og:title` identical to the layout default. Code: `src/app/posts/[id]/page.tsx:157-164`; `src/app/layout.tsx:28-39`. Introduced by c0b05339 (2026-09-28) "fix(security): wave 2A, the database is the boundary (S67)".

### On a laptop, the Report menu has no "This person is under 18" option

**In plain terms** — On a laptop, when a student reports someone from their profile, from a post, or from a direct message, the list of reasons has no "This person is under 18" choice. On a phone that choice is there, and the Privacy Policy says it exists. A student who spots a minor on Vibe and reaches for the laptop has to file it under "Other," which buries a child-safety report in the general pile and makes the policy look untrue to anyone who checks.

**What breaks** — "On my laptop, when I report someone from their profile or from a DM, there's no 'This person is under 18' option, but my phone (and the Privacy Policy) say there is."

**Why it happens** — `public/html/_safetyActions.js:302-311` hardcodes `REPORT_REASONS` with eight codes (`spam`, `harassment`, `sexual`, `intimate_image`, `child_safety`, `hate`, `self_harm`, `other`), renders only that array at `:376` and sends `reason_code: selected` at `:437`. Then stop this class of drift the way the other static twins are protected: a `node --test` parity check that reads both lists, or have the static sheet fetch the reasons from a tiny `GET /api/me/reports/reasons`, or generate the JS array at build time.

**Where** — Live: `curl https://www.connectvibe.app/html/_safetyActions.js` → 200, 25,433 bytes, 0 occurrences of `under_18` (`child_safety` present at line 307), identical to the repo copy. Git: `git log -S under_18 -- src/lib/moderation/reports.ts` = 9077b86 (2026-10-08 11:10 -0400), touching `reports.ts` and `reports.test.ts` only. Live DB: the `reports_reason_code_check` CHECK includes `under_18`. Code: `public/html/_safetyActions.js:302-311`, `:376`, `:437`; `src/lib/moderation/reports.ts:55-65`, `:108-118`; `src/components/safety/ReportSheet.tsx:88`. Introduced by 9077b86 "feat(reports): 'This person is under 18'", which added the reason on the server, in the DB check and in the React sheets but did not touch `public/html/_safetyActions.js`.

### A reaction that didn't go through still shows on the message

**In plain terms** — When a student taps a heart on a chat message and Vibe refuses it, because the message was just removed, the sender has blocked them, or their own account is restricted, the heart still appears on their screen as if it worked. It vanishes the next time they open the chat, so the student thinks reactions are flaky or that someone took theirs away. This happens in club chats and direct messages alike, on phones and laptops.

**What breaks** — "I tapped a heart on a message and it showed up with my highlight, but when I came back later it was gone." Or: "I reacted to a message that had just been removed and it looked like it worked."

**Why it happens** — `src/components/mobile/messages/ConversationView.tsx:577-586` does `await fetch(.../react, {method})` with no `res.ok` check; the rollback lives inside `catch` only, so a non-2xx answer leaves the optimistic reaction in place. `src/components/mobile/messages/club/reactions.ts:29-36` is the same shape ("a rollback when the fetch throws," by its own comment). The route `src/app/api/me/threads/[id]/messages/[messageId]/react/route.ts` answers 404 at `:90` ("That message is no longer there."), 403 blocked at `:98` (commit 4fae889, Oct 8), 503 at `:96` and 500 at `:111`, and the RLS `with_check` on `message_reactions_insert_member` includes `NOT is_restricted_now()`, so a restricted account's insert is refused as well.

**Fix** — Read the response in both helpers: on `!res.ok` roll back exactly as the `catch` branch does and toast the mapped line. `vibeRequest` already does this, so swap the raw `fetch` for `vibeRequest(url, { method, json: { emoji }, failure: "Couldn't add your reaction." })` and branch on `r.ok`. For blocked and 404, also drop the local reaction row so the painted state matches the server.

**Where** — `ConversationView.tsx:577-586`; `club/reactions.ts:29-36`; `react/route.ts:90`, `:96`, `:98`, `:111`; `pg_policies` `message_reactions_insert_member` `with_check` includes `NOT is_restricted_now()`; `git merge-base --is-ancestor 4fae889 origin/main` = yes, so the block refusal is in the live build `dpl_5W4wxnWsM6TupJHMhaoj8mFKHWor`. Introduced by 91a40d7 (2026-09-30) for `ConversationView` and 09e6cc4 (2026-09-30) for `club/reactions.ts`; the 403 blocked answer they ignore landed in 4fae889 (2026-10-08). Confirmed from code and the live policy; a signed-in walk reacting to a removed message or across a block was not performed.

## Quiet failures — outages that look like emptiness

### The "active now" number on the campus page

**In plain terms** — The campus page shows a line like "40 on Vibe · 3 active now." On October 8 the way Vibe records "this person was just here" was changed so that nobody's last-seen time can be read by other students, and the count is now done by the database itself. Everything we can check from the outside says the change is correct, but nobody has yet signed in and watched it work for real, and no one's "last seen" has been recorded since the change. If something in it is off, a student opening the campus page sees "0 active now" (or no number at all) while they are standing right there, and Vibe looks empty and abandoned on exactly the screen that is supposed to feel alive. This still needs one live, signed-in check; four other small changes from the same day (the "Member" starter name, the under-18 report reason, the personal-email row, the yearly school check-in) were looked over at the same time and need nothing.

**What breaks** — "I'm on the campus page right now and it says 0 active now. Is anyone even using this?"

**Why it happens** — `supabase/migrations/20261008120000_users_last_active_private.sql` revoked SELECT on `users.last_active_at` for `authenticated` and added `users_active_count(p_campus_ids)` so the count no longer needs the column to be readable. Two live pieces now lean on grants that changed today and neither has been exercised since. `src/app/api/me/heartbeat/route.ts:27-30` runs `supabase.from("users").update({ last_active_at: ... }).eq("id", user.id)` with the viewer's own session client and no `.select()`; the UPDATE grant was kept (the migration's post-check confirms it), so this should succeed, but if it 500s every heartbeat fails quietly (console.error only) and everyone's last-active clock stops. `src/app/api/stats/campus/route.ts:112-118` calls `supabase.rpc("users_active_count", { p_campus_ids: ids })` and falls back to the old `.gte("last_active_at", activeSince)` read only when the error code is `PGRST202` (function missing); any other error returns `count: null`, and the banner then shows no number. No user row has `last_active_at` on or after Oct 8, so there is no live proof yet of either path. The sibling Oct-8 changes were reviewed in the same pass: `src/lib/profile/onboarding-prefill.ts` (`TRIGGER_FALLBACK_NAME`) and `public/html/onboarding.html:787` handle the "Member" starter name; `src/lib/auth/school-checkin.ts:63-64` exempts rows with a personal email or a non-school sign-in address; `reports_reason_code_check` includes `under_18`. None of those needs a code change.

**Fix** — No code change is known to be needed; this is a verification owed before testers arrive. Sign in as a founder, open /campus, then run (counts only) `select count(*) from public.users where last_active_at > now() - interval '5 minutes'` expecting 1, and GET `/api/stats/campus` expecting `ok: true` with `activeNow >= 1`. If the heartbeat returns 500, the UPDATE policy or grant on `users.last_active_at` for `authenticated` is the place to look. If `activeNow` comes back null instead of a number, check the EXECUTE grant on `users_active_count` for the role the stats route runs as. Also press Run once on the `/api/cron/school-checkin` cron in Vercel and expect `{ok: true, checkins_sent: 0}`.

**Where** — SQL (counts only): users with `last_active_at >= 2026-10-08` = 0; `max(last_active_at)` = 2026-10-06 18:13 UTC; last_active_15m = 0, last_active_7d = 3, last_active_null = 12. `has_column_privilege(authenticated, users.last_active_at, SELECT)` = false, UPDATE = true; SELECT on `id`/`campus_id`/`name` = true; table-level SELECT = false. `supabase_auth_admin` EXECUTE on `handle_new_user` = true; `handle_new_user` md5 `ddb2cf20c06b25fab017b718518f5ba4` with `position('split_part')` = 0 (the email-derived name is gone). `reports_reason_code_check` includes `under_18`. `schema_migrations` top = 20261008140000, with 20261008120000 present. Vercel: live deployment `dpl_5W4w…` = commit 33ce032, which contains d8284bc. Introduced by `20261008120000_users_last_active_private` + d8284bc (2026-10-08). Status: needs a signed-in live test; impact latent until then.

### Pressing "Going" on an event quietly undoes itself

This happens on phone and desktop, but only when the RSVP is refused; a normal RSVP still sticks.

**What breaks** — "I pressed Going on an event and it lit up for a second, then went back to normal. I pressed it again and the same thing happened."

**Why it happens** — src/app/campus/campus-home.tsx:10056-10075, setRsvp does a raw fetch; on `!res.ok` it only toasts when `res.status === 403 && body?.code === 'campus_visiting'`, then throws; the catch at :10077 does console.error plus the counter rollback and no toast. The same EventCard is rendered on the phone by src/components/mobile/CampusMobile.tsx:2184 and on org pages through src/app/orgs/[handle]/OrgEventsSection.tsx:5-10, so all three surfaces share the silence.

**Fix** — Replace the raw fetch in setRsvp with vibeRequest (failure: "Couldn't save your RSVP." / "Couldn't change your RSVP."), keeping campus_visiting as the one special case (quiet: true, then the existing toast). The rollback math stays as is.

**Where** — campus-home.tsx:10004 (export function EventCard), :10056-10085 (setRsvp); rsvp/route.ts at the lines marked fail(...) and tooManyRequests; pg_get_functiondef(rsvp_on_home_campus) matches the route's member exception, so there is no route/function mismatch behind it. Introduced by ed0c0ff8 (2026-05-06) which wrote the silent rollback; ef18f50 (2026-09-27) added the toast for the campus_visiting case only.

### Verifying your school email loses the chat you were tapping into

**In plain terms** — A student who has not yet verified their school email taps a message notification or a shared link. Vibe stops them to verify first, which is right. But once they type the code it forgets where they were headed: they land on the setup screens and then on the campus page instead of the chat or post they tapped, and have to go find it on their own. Phone and desktop alike; it only affects accounts that still need to verify, which today is six accounts, and all six have also not finished setup.
