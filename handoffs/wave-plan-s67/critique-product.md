# Critique — product lens (S67 plan)

Read: plan.md, waves.md, contracts.md, the five reader reports, `handoffs/2026-09-25-founders-meeting-product-notes.md`, and the code the findings cite. 2026-09-25.

## Verdict

The plan maps all five of Franky's asks to a wave and drops nothing he said: widgets (wave 2), desktop feed like the phone (wave 1), dark mode (waves 3 + 5), the rolling strip (wave 1), view other campuses + 45-day lock (wave 4). The "rolling newsletter only on the campus feed" reading is right: Feed tab only, phone and desktop, the Otto tab's own "Coming up" untouched, the Events tab loses Heads up. "View other campuses" is right where his words are clear: follows, profiles and DMs stay global; RSVP is refused away from home. The additions are mostly the plumbing those asks need (event anchor, `?campus=` on trending, Skills as a phone section) and none is large. One blocker: dark mode defaults to "match system" while the wave that finishes the desktop profile, messages and onboarding pages is marked cuttable, so students who never opted in would land on half-dark pages. The bigger product risk is wave 4, where the plan settles four things Franky's words do not settle (refusing club joins, opening all 11 campuses across both universities, hiding the Chat tab, leaving search on the home campus) without asking him. Everything else is a reasonable default with copy to tighten. "Video posts" is used correctly throughout; no "clips" in new copy.

## Ask Franky before wave 4 (five, with the plan's default)

1. Refuse club joins while visiting (default: refuse, no request) — or keep Request-to-join and only block RSVP? His words cover RSVP and chat, not join. (#2)
2. Which campuses are viewable (default: all 11, both universities, Map included)? The meeting notes said "nearby / within a set distance", and the Map of a closed campus names its few students. (#3, #6)
3. Desktop feed: centered 720px column (default) or cards at full width? He said "almost" like mobile. (#9)
4. Does "rolling" mean moving? Default: auto-roll when overflowing, which on the phone is always. (#8)
5. Dark mode default: "match system" (default) or opt-in "light" until the desktop legacy pages are fully dark? (#1)

Not worth asking: Posts can't be hidden, moving and hiding are free, empty widgets hidden for visitors, 14-day RSVP window, Events tab loses Heads up, `?campus=all` stays own-university. All match his words or the S61/S65 decisions on record.
