# Research briefs for James and Rylan (drafted Oct 13, 2026)

Two short research jobs that came out of Franky's meeting with Kaki. One
each, same questions, so the two can be compared side by side. The main focus
is learning who pays these companies and what students really use them for.

Suggested due date: Sunday, October 18 (Franky to confirm).
Format: one page each, plain notes, links to where each fact came from.

## James: Lounge

Why you: it is a business-model and marketing question, which is your field.

What we already heard: undergrads use Lounge for free, and national
fraternity and sorority organizations pay for the safety and data side.

Find out:
1. What does the product do for a student, and for a chapter officer?
2. Who pays them, how much if it is public, and what do those payers get?
3. Which national organizations or campuses use it? Any at IU or Purdue?
4. How do they market it: to students, to nationals, to universities?
5. What do students say about it (app store reviews, Reddit, people in SAE
   or on IFC who have used it)?
6. What do they do well, and what do they do badly?
7. Where is Vibe clearly different, in one or two sentences?

## Rylan: Rally, and the other IU Innovates startup

Why you: it is on-campus and people-first, and it needs good questions asked.

What we already heard: Caroline Kramer is building Rally through IU Innovates
in Bloomington, with an event-booking and Greek-life angle. Kaki offered to
introduce us. There is at least one more similar startup inside IU Innovates.

Find out:
1. What does Rally do today, and who is it for?
2. Is it live? Roughly how many students or chapters use it?
3. How does it make money, or plan to?
4. What is the other similar IU Innovates startup, and what does it do?
5. Ask five students on campus: what do you use now to find events and to
   run your club or floor (Instagram, GroupMe, something else), and what
   annoys you about it?
6. Would a founder-to-founder talk with Caroline be worth taking? What three
   questions would we ask her?
7. Where is Vibe clearly different, in one or two sentences?
