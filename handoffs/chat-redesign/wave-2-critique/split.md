## Verdict

**The split is buildable as written, and its proof is real and runs in the cloud.** The file plan of 10.1 assigns all 51 top-level declarations of `MessagesMobile.tsx` to exactly one file, no private name is used across files, the import graph has no cycle, and the two re-exports keep `MessagesSwitch.tsx:4` and `CampusMobile.tsx:37` (with the type-level `Parameters<typeof ConversationView>` at `:2471`) compiling unchanged. What needs fixing is around the proof and the batches after it: the checker lets an ADDED module-level statement through and the contract never says what to do when it reports a difference; the split's proof is listed as a gate of EVERY batch although the edits of 12.1 and 10.2.5 make it fail from W2-B3 on; the one-line stub skip of 12.1 is placed before `MessageBubble`'s hooks and would add a `rules-of-hooks` lint error; and three mount points (`ClubSpace`, `CardBubble`, `ReactionPicker.onRemove`) are assigned to one batch in 13.1 and to another in 11.3 / 11.8 / 13.2.

Counts: 0 HIGH, 6 MEDIUM, 7 LOW.

**The top changes**

1. **Make the checker's verdict complete and say what a difference means** (M1, M2): ADDED is fatal too (a new module-level statement is a behaviour change the checker allows today); the split's proof is W2-S's gate alone, re-run once at integration with the three expected CHANGED names; a non-empty report means the split is wrong, never the checker, the base or the original text.
2. **Move the stub skip after the last hook** (M3): `if (message.hidden) return null;` goes after `MessageBubble`'s fourth `useState` (today `:4035`), not "at the top of the component", or `eslint` fails W2-B3 with `react-hooks/rules-of-hooks`.
3. **One creator per mount file** (M4, M5): `club/ClubSpace.tsx` is created by W2-S2 and `cards/CardBubble.tsx` by W2-S3 (their owners), each as that batch's first commit with the pinned props; `ReactionPicker.onRemove` joins W2-S2's first commit because W2-S3's `CardBubble` needs it.
4. **Say who reads the block set when `toChatMessages` is called directly** (M6): W2-B4's `board_latest` calls `toChatMessages` with no `blocked`; 9.1 only makes `readChatMessages` / `readChatMessage` read it. Either `toChatMessages` reads it when absent, or 7.2.4 passes `blockedIdsFor(...)`.
