## Findings

### VIS-S2-1 — MEDIUM — Up next line 1 clips the RSVP word to `● M…` at 375
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/UpNext.tsx:107-118`
- **what**: In a 2-column card (167.5px outer, 147.5px inside at 375) `Tonight · 9:30 PM · ● Maybe` is ≈160px at 12px/600 with the 5px flex gaps, so the word ellipsises to `M…` in every space shot (07, 09, 10, 21; both themes). S2-repair chose to keep the time whole and let the word yield (STOPPED 10); the result reads as a defect next to the mockup's clean `Tonight 7:30 · ✓ Going`. The `Live now · ✓ Going` card fits.
- **fix**: two changes, both in the line-1 span. (a) Tighten so the common case fits: `fontSize: 11.5, letterSpacing: -0.15, gap: 4`, and render the separator inside the time span (`{upNextLine(e, now)} ·`) instead of its own gapped span (saves two gaps; ≈147px for `Tonight · 9:30 PM · ● Maybe`). (b) Never show a cut word: swap which side yields — the RSVP group gets `flexShrink: 0` (the word whole, as the mark) and the time span gets `minWidth: 0, overflow: "hidden", textOverflow: "ellipsis"`, so an overlong case reads `Tonight · 9:30 P… · ● Maybe` rather than `● M…`. Alternative worth an open point: the mockup's own line-1 shape for the 2-column card, `Tonight 9:30 PM · ✓ Going` (`eventTime` with the ` · ` dropped). Re-shoot 07 at 375 to confirm `Maybe` is whole.

### VIS-S2-2 — MEDIUM — A card post on the Board is drawn as a Lounge bubble, not full width in the post
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/BoardPost.tsx:196-199` (and `cards/CardBubble.tsx:213-247`, W2-S3)
- **what**: 11.4 pins "a card when `kind !== "text"` (11.8, full width inside the post)". The shots (09, 10) show the Event card as `CardBubble`'s Lounge layout: a 292px card pushed right behind a second 28px `PS` avatar (the byline already shows one), the `18px 18px 18px 6px` tail radius, 8px gap — inside a 351px post it leaves a 47px dead column and repeats the author. Known as request R5 (S2-repair) to W2-S3; still on screen.
- **fix**: `CardBubble` takes `layout?: "bubble" | "post"`; for `"post"`: no avatar column, the card `width: "100%"`, `borderRadius: 14`, the header line kept (`Priya Shah · Event` is the card's own line, 1.2). `BoardPost.tsx:198` passes `layout="post"`. Until W2-S3 lands it, the wrapper at `:197` can at least hide the avatar column: `<div style={{ marginTop: 8, marginLeft: -36 }}>` is a hack — prefer the prop.

### VIS-S2-3 — MEDIUM — The empty club's block sits 12px too far in and 8px too low
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/ClubEmpty.tsx:70`
- **what**: `ClubEmpty` is rendered as `emptyBlock` inside `RoomThread`'s list box, which already has `padding: "8px 12px 8px"` (RoomThread.tsx:303); the section adds `padding: "16px 16px 12px"`, so the heading and the rows start at 28px from the edge (the mockup: 16px, W0_EmptyClub:45 `padding:0 16px 12px`) and the coral row is 319px wide instead of ≈343 at 375; the bottom gap is 20px instead of 12.
- **fix**: `padding: "16px 4px 4px"` on the section (12 + 4 = 16 each side; 8 + 4 = 12 at the bottom).

### VIS-S2-4 — LOW — The chip row overflows at 375 with the last chip cut at the edge
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/RoomChips.tsx:220-223, 261`
- **what**: Lounge · Board● · 🔒Officers · 📅Fall Formal is ≈386px with the mockup's `0 12px` padding and 6px gaps; at 390 the mockup fits, at 375 the fourth chip is clipped by the viewport with no scroll affordance (07, 09, 10, 21). It scrolls, so nothing is lost.
- **fix**: `gap: 5` and inactive pill padding `"0 11px"` (`"0 11px 0 9px"` with an icon, `"0 9px 0 11px"` with the dot) recover ≈9px so the four common chips fit; keep `overflowX: "auto"` for a fifth.

### VIS-S2-5 — LOW — The RSVP pill stays tappable on a closed event chat
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/EventAbout.tsx:369-380`
- **what**: 12 (`Closed`) still shows the `Going` pill and opens the sheet; 11.8 hides a closed card's RSVP pills ("nothing to join"); 11.6 does not say either way.
- **fix**: when `event.state === "closed"` render the pill's inner `<span>` alone (no `<button>`, `aria-label` dropped) so the status still reads but nothing opens; or `aria-disabled` + no `onClick`.

### VIS-S2-6 — LOW — The club skeleton's chip pills are 36px where the chips are 30px
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/ClubSpace.tsx:496-498`
- **what**: `ClubBodySkeleton` draws three 36px pills in the 45px row; the real chips are 30px pills, so the row jumps 6px when the read answers.
- **fix**: `height: 30` and the row `padding: "7px 12px 8px"` (same 45px).

### VIS-S2-7 — LOW — The Board's reply-field send has no 44px visual affordance when enabled in Light
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/BoardPost.tsx:266`
- **what**: The `Send reply` arrow is `t.coralText` (`#C8401E`) when enabled and `t.muted` when empty on a transparent 44px button — fine by the mockup (W0_Board:91 is a bare arrow) and by contrast (6.3:1 on `#FAF7F2`); only the empty state's grey arrow (`#5A564F`, 6.8:1) looks identical in weight to the enabled one at a glance in Light. Polish only.
- **fix**: `opacity: canSend ? 1 : 0.55` on the button, or keep as is.
