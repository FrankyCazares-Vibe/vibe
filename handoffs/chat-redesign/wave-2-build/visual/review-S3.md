## Findings

### VIS-S3-1 — MEDIUM — The sheets' `<club> · <room>` line never gets its club dot
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/RoomComposer.tsx:324-330` (the `ComposeSheets` mount; the prop exists on `compose/ComposeSheets.tsx:565` and `compose/CardSheet.tsx:337, 404`)
- **what**: `W0_NewPoll:73` (and the two other sheets, MK:9-11 "chrome as section 9") draw a 14px club dot in the club's colour before `Salsa Club · the Lounge`. `CardSheet` draws it only when `orgId` is passed; `RoomComposer` passes `clubName` and `orgHandle` but no id, so every sheet in 15–19 (both themes) shows the bare line. Known as S3-DESIGN-9 → REQUESTS 1 (an S2 file); still on screen.
- **fix**: `RoomComposer` takes `orgId: string` beside `orgHandle` (`RoomComposer.tsx:53-54`, the props type) and passes `orgId={orgId}` to `<ComposeSheets …>` at `:324`; `ClubSpace.tsx:389` passes `orgId={org.id}` and `EventChat.tsx:168` passes `orgId={club.id}` (both already hold the `OrgRef`). `clubGradient(orgId)` then colours the dot as the header avatar. Re-shoot 15 to confirm the dot sits 6px before the text on the 12.5px line.

### VIS-S3-2 — MEDIUM — The New sign-up slot input cuts a 17-character label at 375
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/compose/NewSignupSheet.tsx:299, 309, 290, 293` and `compose/CardSheet.tsx:550` (`RemoveButton`)
- **what**: The row is `343 − 8 − 118 − 8 − 36 = 173px` for the input at 375 (16px gutters, 8px gaps, the 118px stepper, the X at 44 − 8), i.e. 145px of text after the 14px paddings; `Leaving from Union` at 16px/600 is ≈ 150px, so 17 shows `Leaving from Unior` and `Leaving from Ball H` with the last glyph cut at the padding edge (17-new-signup-*, both themes). The mockup's labels are 8–9 characters (`Mon 11–12`) and its input is ≈ 220px (no X column); the contract adds the X, which took the width. A real slot label (`Leaves 6:45`, `Decorations 5–6 PM`) sits right at the edge.
- **fix**: recover ≈ 24px on the row: the stepper `width: 106, padding: 0` (`:309`; the arms stay 44×44 and the number `minWidth: 18`), the row `gap: 6` (`:299`), and `RemoveButton` `width: 40, marginRight: -12` (`CardSheet.tsx:550`; the 44px hit is kept by `height: 44` and the −12 into the gutter, as the poll sheet's X). Then the input is `343 − 6 − 106 − 6 − 28 = 197px` (169px of text: `Leaving from Union` fits whole). Mirror the header: `Spots` column `width: 106` (`:290`) and the spacer `width: 28` (`:293`).

### VIS-S3-3 — LOW — The "+" is not inverted while the tray is open
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/club/RoomComposer.tsx:297`
- **what**: `W0_Tray:93` turns the "+" into a close button with `border: 1px solid #F6F1EA; background: #F6F1EA; color: #0C0B0B` (the active-chip inversion) and an X icon. The app keeps the surface disc and rotates the plus 45° (14-tray-*: a dark disc with a light X in Night, a white disc with a dark X in Light). The rotated plus reads as an X, so nothing is unclear; the inverted disc is the mockup's cue that the tray is the "+"'s own state.
- **fix**: on the button's style: `background: trayOpen ? t.activeChip.bg : t.surface, color: trayOpen ? t.activeChip.ink : t.ink, border: trayOpen ? `1px solid ${t.activeChip.bg}` : <today's border>` (Light inverts to `#141416` / `#FAF7F2`, as the active chip). Keep the rotation.

### VIS-S3-4 — LOW — The Share sheet's note counter sits alone on a row
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/compose/ShareEventSheet.tsx:511-513`
- **what**: The poll and sign-up sheets put the counter at the right of a label (`Question`, `Title`); the note block has no label word in 1.2, so `26 / 200` (`0 / 200` when empty, 19) stands alone right-aligned on a 16px row above the field — it reads as a stray. The mockup has no counter (`W0_ShareEvent:102-105`); 1.2 adds one, without saying where.
- **fix**: drop the row and draw the counter inside the field's right end: wrap the input in `position: relative`, give the input `paddingRight: 72`, and place the `<Counter>` absolutely at `right: 14, top: 0, height: 48, display: flex, alignItems: center`. The `aria-label="<n> of 200 characters"` stays.

### VIS-S3-5 — LOW — The poll sheet's option pills change width when the third option arrives
- **where**: `src/components/mobile/messages/compose/NewPollSheet.tsx:127`
- **what**: At the minimum of 2 the Remove X is not rendered, so the two pills run the full 343px (15); tapping `Add option` mounts an X on every row and all three pills shrink by 36px (16). The rows jump; the mockup draws every row with the X column.
- **fix**: keep the column when at the minimum: `{options.length > POLL_OPTIONS_MIN ? <RemoveButton … /> : <span aria-hidden style={{ width: 36, flexShrink: 0 }} />}` (the sign-up sheet's header already reserves the column this way, `NewSignupSheet.tsx:293`).
